# Review prompt, factoid run

Read the ontology, vocabulary and annotation guidelines of the contract folder, the complete source packets and the candidate Turtle files. Do not read the extractors' batch reports before you have judged.

Compare every file with `summary_plain`. Report unsupported factoids, omitted central in-scope passages, wrong actor identifications or roles, addressees treated as participants, temporal overreach, an earlier act collapsed into its confirmation, provisions read as performance, paraphrased literals, lost conditions or exceptions, and identifications taken from another packet. Matching evidence text proves that the words exist, not that they support the factoid.

Write record-specific findings with category, severity, subject, description, exact source passage and proposed correction, and a coverage note for every record, also where you find nothing. Do not change the Turtle files or the contract. The review is agentic feedback and neither expert acceptance nor a measurement of accuracy.
