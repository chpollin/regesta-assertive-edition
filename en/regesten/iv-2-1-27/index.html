The candidate carries only role p_komment, not nennung, which supports reading the summary's duke as the ruler; the extractor flagged the identification as a reading. Defensible, but it rests on the candidate role, not on the summary text.
  • Finding Konrad von Rietfeld (K000502) and Berengar von Albeck (B000529) are named in the summary and have matching candidates, but appear only inside the rf:ground literal, so the graph cannot find them. The oppression has no act type (rv:Dispute does not fit), and no role covers an oppressor in a reason clause.
  • Finding The protected Ministerialien are rv:Beneficiary and the duke rv:Recipient; this is a reasonable reading (the duke receives the charge, the Ministerialien benefit), but the Latin 'in manus ... commisit' makes the Ministerialien the object handed over. rf:refersToAct is defined for confirmations or assurances referring to an earlier act; here it links a consequent act to its precondition, a stretch of the property. Typing the bishop's handover of persons as rv:Grant (transfer of a right, possession or institution) is also an approximation.
  • Adjudication, kept F000681 is the father ('Herzog von Schwaben, Vater Friedrich Barbarossas') and carries only role p_komment in this packet, while the regest belongs to the Barbarossa regesta and the preceding record 22653 of the same series reports the son's succession in the duchy. The identification with the shared ruler is defensible; the file's comment already marks it as a reading, which the corrector keeps as an open identity.
  • Adjudication, recorded as a gap in the model 'wegen ihrer Bedrückung durch Konrad von Rietfeld und Berengar von Albeck' names them as the cause of the handover, not as parties to it. Adding them as rv:Participant to the handover factoid would assert participation in the bishop's transfer that the passage does not state. The oppression itself has no act type and its perpetrators no role; the ground literal keeps the exact wording.
  • Adjudication, kept The duke receives the charge ('in manus Friderici ducis Suevię ... commisit') and the Ministerialien are those placed under protection, so Recipient and Beneficiary read the passage correctly. rf:refersToAct is defined for 'a later act such as a confirmation or assurance' that refers to an earlier act of the same regest; 'die genannten Bamberger Ministerialien, die ihm ... übergeben worden sind' is such a reference, and no date is asserted. rv:Grant is the nearest available type for the handover; a person-handover type is a gap for the next version, not a correction.
