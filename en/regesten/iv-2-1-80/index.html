RI IV,2,1 n. 801152 (Ende April)Soest (Sosatie)

TEI Turtle JSON

Friedrich bestätigt den Klöstern Liesborn (ecclesie sanctorum Cosme et Damiani in Lisborn) und St. Maria in Überwasser zu Münster (ecclesie sancte Marie in Monasterio) in Nachahmung König Konrads (III.) ihre im königlichen Gebiet um Remagen gelegenen Güter, nämlich in Lohrsdorf (Lorstorp), Gimersheim und Green (Grien). Z.: (Erz)bischof Arnold II. von Köln, Propst Ulrich von Soest, Albert (Domdekan von Köln), Heinrich (Kanzleinotar); die Fürsten Markgraf Albrecht (der Bär), die Grafen Albert von Nörvenich (Ormenech) und Heinrich von Arnsberg; die Edelfreien Dietrich von Hachen, Ruthard von Rodenberg (Rathardus de Ruderberge), Vogt Walter von Soest, Ionatas von Volmarstein (Volmunstene), Philipp von Dortmund (Tremonia); die Ministerialen Vogt Hermann von Köln, Heinrich von Volmarstein, Adolf von Gürzenich, Amalrich von Wormersdorf (Vurmestorp), Thimo und Marsilius von Soest, Gottschalk von Paderborn. - Arnoldus canc. vice Heinrici Mogontini archiep. et archicapellani ; in Wiederholung des DK. III. 249 wahrscheinlich außerhalb der Kanzlei mundiert. Quotiens religiosorum virorum .

Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The passage says only that Friedrich acts in imitation of König Konrad (III.). Typing Konrad's act as rv:Confirmation is a plausible reading but not stated; the regest does not say what Konrad did. The qualifier "in Nachahmung" describes the relation of Friedrich's act to Konrad's, not Konrad's act itself, so it sits on the wrong factoid.
  • Finding "ihre ... Güter" states that the goods belong to the monasteries. A rv:Possession state factoid could record this; the confirmation covers it implicitly.
  • Adjudication, corrected In 'Friedrich bestätigt ... in Nachahmung König Konrads (III.) ihre ... Güter' the phrase qualifies Friedrich's confirmation. That Konrad's act was itself a confirmation is the natural reading of a confirmation made in imitation of him and stays, with the gap noted.
  • Adjudication, not required The possessive stands inside the confirmed object; the guidelines do not require a separate state factoid, and a confirmation establishes no possession by itself.
  • Correction Moved rf:qualifier "in Nachahmung" from urn:regesta:22707:earlier-act-konrad to urn:regesta:22707:confirmation; both factoids' producer set to correction-g4.
  • Correction Earlier act's type rv:Confirmation, evidence and reference kept.
  • Gap in the model, extraction 'in Nachahmung König Konrads (III.)' implies an earlier act by Konrad III whose content and recipients the regest does not state; recorded as a bare earlier Confirmation with qualifier 'in Nachahmung', and whether imitation of a predecessor's charter is a confirmation of an earlier act is an open reading.
  • Gap in the model, extraction 'ihre ... Güter' not extracted as Possession, because the possessive stands only inside the confirmed object.
  • Gap in the model, review No property for an act modelled on an earlier ruler's act (Nachahmung) without asserting what that earlier act was.
