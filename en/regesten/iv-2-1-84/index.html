Friedrich bestätigt gemäß den Privilegien seiner Vorgänger, Lothar III. und Konrad III., die volle Unterordnung des Klosters Hastière (Hasteriensis) unter die Abtei Waulsort (Walcidorensis). Z.: die Bischöfe Bernhard von Hildesheim, Wichmann von Zeitz, Anselm von Havelberg, Abt Wibald von Stablo und Corvey, Herzog Heinrich von Sachsen, Markgraf Albrecht von Brandenburg, dominus Welf, der Oheim des Königs, Pfalzgraf Otto von Wittelsbach, Graf Ulrich von Lenzburg, Markward von Grumbach. - Arnoldus canc. vice Heinrici Moguntini archiep. et archicanc. ; in Wiederholung des DK. III. 251 (VU. I), erweitert um einen Passus über die vermögensrechtliche Verfügungsgewalt des Abtes von Waulsort über Hastière aus dem kurz vorher entstandenen, unechten DL. III. 131 (VU. II), wohl mundiert von Wibald. Ad imperialem munificentiam .

Original dating (VIII idus maii, Goslarie)

Transmission Kop.: Abschrift von ca. 1525 im Liber monasteriorum Walsiodorensis et Hasteriensis f. 118, Seminarbibliothek Namur (C); Beglaubigte Abschrift von 1735 im Liber quartus cartarum von Waulsort f. 128, Staatsarchiv Namur (D). Drucke: Stumpf, Acta ined. 145 no 118; Despy, Chartes de Waulsort 1, 361 no 28; MG. DF. I. 9. Reg.: Wauters, Table chronol. 7/1, 255 zu Mai 5; Stumpf 3624.

Commentary Erwirkt von Wibald, der seit seiner Profeß in engen Beziehungen zu Waulsort stand, vgl. die Vorbemerkungen zu DL. III. 131 und DK. III. 251 sowie auch Hausmann, Reichskanzlei 234; für eine Mundierung durch Wibald spricht vor allem das in D nachgezeichnete Monogramm, das dem in dem von ihm mundierten DF. I. 1 (s. Reg. 67) entspricht.

Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding Waulsort is referenced as rv:Beneficiary. The confirmed subordination is a right of Waulsort over Hastière, so Waulsort is the party to whom something is confirmed, which the vocabulary defines as rv:Recipient. Beneficiary is not wrong but weaker than the passage allows.
  • Finding The two earlier privilege factoids carry only the grantor. The passage says the confirmation follows these privileges, so the earlier acts concern the same subordination; without rf:concerns and without Waulsort they are free-floating grants of unknown object. The rf:Grant type is a generic reading of 'Privilegien' that the passage does not narrow; this is acceptable, but the object link is recoverable. Same for urn:regesta:22711:privilege-konrad.
  • Finding The witness list carries a stated kinship ('dominus Welf, der Oheim des Königs'). The guidelines exclude formulaic witness lists, so the omission is contract-conform, but a kinship word inside Z.: is not formulaic and would otherwise be an rv:Kinship factoid.
  • Adjudication, kept The passage names no dative recipient ('bestätigt ... die volle Unterordnung des Klosters Hastière (Hasteriensis) unter die Abtei Waulsort'); the confirmation is in Waulsort's favour, which rv:Beneficiary states without asserting receipt.
  • Adjudication, corrected The confirmation of the subordination is made 'gemäß den Privilegien seiner Vorgänger, Lothar III. und Konrad III.', so the passage itself ties the earlier privileges to the same subordination; the right node already exists in the file.
  • Correction Added rf:concerns <urn:regesta:22711:right-unterordnung> to <urn:regesta:22711:privilege-lothar> and <urn:regesta:22711:privilege-konrad>; both factoids now attributed to correction-g5. Type, grantor references and evidence unchanged; no date, no recipient.
  • Gap in the model, extraction The earlier privileges of Lothar III. and Konrad III. carry no stated act type; typed with the top concept rv:Grant as a placeholder.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No right type for the subordination of one monastery under another abbey; rv:LordshipRights (top concept) used as the nearest group.
  • Gap in the model, extraction Witness list states 'dominus Welf, der Oheim des Königs' (kinship); not extracted because the guidelines exclude formulaic witness lists.
  • Gap in the model, review The relation of predecessor in office ('seiner Vorgänger, Lothar III. und Konrad III.') has no state type or role; rv:OfficeHolding cannot express succession.
  • Gap in the model, review Whether kinship statements inside witness lists are in scope is not settled by the guidelines.
