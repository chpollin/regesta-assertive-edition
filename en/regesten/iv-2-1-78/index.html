RI IV,2,1 n. 781152 April 20Köln

TEI Turtle JSON

Friedrich stellt der Abtei Laach (sancte Marię de Lacu) den ihr von ihrem Gründer, Pfalzgraf Heinrich, geschenkten Hof Bendorf (Bedendorf) zurück, indem er den widerrechtlichen Besitzer Heinrich von Molsberg (Henricus quidam de Mollesperch), dem der Hof schon durch ein Hofgerichtsurteil unter Konrad III. abgesprochen worden ist, durch die Zahlung von 60 Mark entschädigt; dies wird von Erzbischof Arnold II. von Köln, dessen Kirche dieses Gut gehört, in Gegenwart des Königs und der Fürsten kraft seiner und der päpstlichen Gewalt bestätigt (auctoritate beati Petri et Eugenii secundi sacrosancte Romanę ecclesię pontificis et suo banno). Z.: Erzbischof Arnold II. von Köln, Dompropst Walter von St. Peter zu Köln, die Pröpste Gerhard von Bonn, Tiepold von Xanten, Domdekan Albert von Köln, die Äbte Markward von Fulda, Heinrich von Hersfeld, Nikolaus von Siegburg (Sibergensis), Herzog Heinrich von Sachsen, Markgraf Albrecht (der Bär), die Herzoge Welf, Heinrich von Limburg, die Grafen Ulrich von Lenzburg (Linceborch), Dietrich von Ahr, Heinrich von Namur, Wilhelm und sein Bruder Gerhard von Jülich, Adolf von Saffenberg und sein Sohn Hermann, Adolf von Berg, Ludwig von Looz, Robert von Laurenburg (Nassau/Lurenborch), (Graf) Eberhard von Sayn und sein Bruder (Heinrich), Graf (Heinrich) von Diez (Dyedesa), Graf Siegfried von Wied und sein Bruder Burchard von Wied, Arnold und Gerhard von Blankenheim, Hermann von Kuik, Friedrich von Arnsberg, Rembold von Isenburg und Hartbernus de Bocstella . - Arnoldus canc. vice Maguntini archiep. et archicanc. ; unter Kölner Einfluß von dem an der Kirche geschulten Notar romanischer Zunge Arnold II. C, der hier erstmals auftritt, verfaßt und offenbar auch mundiert. Ex commisso nobis .

Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The restitution returns the very estate that the earlier donation conveyed (den ihr ... geschenkten Hof), and it rests on the earlier judgment that had denied the estate to Heinrich von Molsberg. Neither the donation nor the judgment is linked to the restitution, so the dependency the regest states is lost; the donation and judgment float as unconnected acts.
  • Finding "dessen Kirche dieses Gut gehört" states ownership by the Cologne church while the abbey is the one receiving the estate. rv:Possession is the closest state type, but it flattens the distinction between ownership and holding that the regest implies; Arnold, through whom the church is introduced (dessen), is not referenced.
  • Finding "kraft seiner und der päpstlichen Gewalt" names the authority on which the confirmation rests. It is stored as rf:qualifier; rf:ground would also fit. The papal authority is not linked to any actor, which is correct since the German text names no pope.
  • Finding Pfalzgraf Heinrich is called the founder of the abbey (ihrem Gründer). The contract has no act or state type for foundation, so it cannot be expressed beyond the donation.
  • Adjudication, corrected 'den ihr von ihrem Gründer, Pfalzgraf Heinrich, geschenkten Hof Bendorf (Bedendorf) zurück' states that the restitution returns what the earlier donation gave; rf:refersToAct is defined for a later act referring to an earlier act of the same regest. The passage does not say the restitution rests on the court judgment, so that link is not added.
  • Adjudication, corrected 'dessen Kirche dieses Gut gehört' states belonging, while the vocabulary only has rv:Possession; adding the exact word as rf:qualifier keeps the distinction in the source's wording without extending the model.
  • Adjudication, recorded as a gap in the model The contract has no act or state type for founding, and rf:qualifier is meant for wording that qualifies the factoid's type or time, not a characterisation of the grantor.
  • Adjudication, kept rf:ground is a reason the regest gives for the act; 'kraft ... Gewalt' names the authority under which it is done, which is no reason. The exact wording is kept either way.
  • Correction urn:regesta:22705:restitution: added rf:refersToAct <urn:regesta:22705:donation>; no link to judgment-hofgericht; producer correction-g4.
  • Correction urn:regesta:22705:possession-kirche-koeln: added rf:qualifier "gehört"; producer correction-g4.
  • Gap in the model, extraction Founding relation ('ihrem Gründer, Pfalzgraf Heinrich') has no act or state type; not recorded beyond the donation.
  • Gap in the model, extraction Role of Konrad III. in the earlier Hofgericht judgment ('unter Konrad III.') is recorded as Participant, because 'unter' does not establish him as judge.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No property for the authority basis of an act; 'kraft seiner und der päpstlichen Gewalt' is kept in rf:qualifier rather than rf:ground.
  • Gap in the model, extraction The regest says the court belongs to the Cologne church and was donated to Laach by its founder; the model cannot express the relation between these tenures (e.g. a fief or a superior right), so both are recorded side by side without a link.
  • Gap in the model, extraction The payment is typed Payment from the reported compensation 'durch die Zahlung von 60 Mark entschädigt'; no compensation type exists.
  • Gap in the model, review No state type distinguishing ownership (gehört) from possession or holding.
  • Gap in the model, review No act or state type for the foundation of an ecclesiastical institution (Gründer).
  • Gap in the model, review No role for presence at an act (in Gegenwart des Königs und der Fürsten); rv:Participant is the fallback.
  • Open identity The pope named only in the Latin quotation (Eugenii secundi ... pontificis) and as 'päpstlichen Gewalt' has no candidate and is not referenced; his authority is kept as the qualifier wording only.
