Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The order is in favour of Wibald's side: the fief was alienated from "seinen Leuten", most plausibly Wibald's people, and the letter is addressed to Wibald. No beneficiary reference is given, so the factoid loses for whom restitution is ordered.
  • Finding The count palatine, who is ordered to restore, carries rv:Addressee. This matches the definition (to whom an order is directed) but loses that he is the party obliged to restore; rv:Obligor is a possible alternative.
  • Finding The summons of Wibald to Köln for 1 November is not extracted. As travel to an assembly it falls under the exclusion, but 22829 extracts a royal summons as rv:Communication, so the run is inconsistent.
  • Finding The alienated fief is typed crm:E18_Physical_Thing; a Lehen is a tenure relation as much as a thing. Acceptable under the contract's thing and right split.
  • Adjudication, corrected conservatively 'das seinen Leuten entfremdete Lehen zurückzustellen' names the people to whom the fief is to be restored, which is central to the order. The possessive 'seinen' is not determinate in the passage, so identifying them with Wibald or his people would be a reading; a regest-local group in the source wording is safe.
  • Adjudication, kept 'er habe dem Pfalzgrafen ... befohlen' directs an order to him; rv:Addressee fits its definition and, unlike rv:Obligor, does not imply that an obligation is established beyond the order.
  • Adjudication, not required 'beruft ihn für den 1. November nach Köln' calls Wibald to a court gathering without a stated dispute; adjudications g5 and g6 treated such summonses to a court day as not required, and the run types a summons as rv:Dispute only where a legal proceeding against the summoned is stated.
  • Correction Declared <urn:regesta:actor:22826:local:leute> a crm:E74_Group with label "seinen Leuten" and added it to <urn:regesta:22826:restitution-order> as rv:Beneficiary with nameInSource "seinen Leuten"; not identified with Wibald; producer of the restitution order set to correction-g10.
  • Correction <urn:regesta:22826:communication> unchanged.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No act type for a command or order to restore; the order is recorded as rv:Enactment with qualifier 'befohlen', not as rv:Restitution, since performance is not stated
  • Gap in the model, extraction No role for the person commanded; the Pfalzgraf carries rv:Addressee
  • Gap in the model, review No way to express the prior alienation (entfremdet) of a fief except as part of a thing label.
  • Open identity 'seinen Leuten': the possessor of 'seinen' (Wibald/Corvey or the Pfalzgraf) is ambiguous, so no beneficiary reference was set
  • Open identity 'beruft ihn für den 1. November nach Köln': 'ihn' may be Wibald or the Pfalzgraf; the summons was not extracted
