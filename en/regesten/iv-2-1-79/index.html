Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The local actor for the judging princes carries rdfs:label and rf:nameInSource "Fürstenspruch". That word names the verdict, not a group of persons; the regest never names the princes as actors. Using it as the actor's name in source misrepresents the wording that identifies the actor.
  • Finding "Veräußerungen" is typed rv:Grant without a recipient. The regest states that the predecessor alienated goods but not to whom or in what form; rv:Grant is the closest act type and does not overstate.
  • Finding rf:refersToAct links the judgment to the alienations, but the judgment rules against them (entgegen). The link is correct in direction, but the oppositional relation is not expressible, and it must not be read as a confirmation of the alienations.
  • Adjudication, corrected conservatively 'Vor Friedrich wird ein Fürstenspruch gefällt' never names the princes; rf:nameInSource and rdfs:label must be the wording naming the actor, and inferring a group from a compound noun is not supported. The safe fix keeps the word on the judgment.
  • Adjudication, corrected The judgment confirms inalienability 'entgegen den Veräußerungen seines Vorgängers Friedrich'. Without the exact wording the link reads neutrally or as approval; adding it as qualifier keeps the opposition in the source's words.
  • Correction urn:regesta:22706:judgment: removed reference <urn:regesta:22706:judgment-ref-fuersten>, deleted that reference node and the actor node <urn:regesta:actor:22706:local:fuersten>; Friedrich and Arnold references unchanged.
  • Correction urn:regesta:22706:judgment: added rf:qualifier "Fürstenspruch" and "entgegen den Veräußerungen" beside "Unveräußerlichkeit"; rf:refersToAct <urn:regesta:22706:alienations> kept; producer correction-g4.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No act type for an unspecified alienation ('Veräußerungen'); typed with the top concept Grant and the exact word kept in rf:qualifier.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No right type or status for inalienability ('Unveräußerlichkeit'); kept as qualifier of the judgment, with the Tafelgüter as the concerned thing.
  • Gap in the model, extraction The predecessor relation in office ('seines Vorgängers') has no state type and is not recorded.
  • Gap in the model, review No property for an act that rules against or invalidates an earlier act without being a formal revocation.
  • Gap in the model, review No act type for alienation in general (Veräußerung) distinct from grant or sale.
  • Gap in the model, review Inalienability as a legal status of goods has no right or state type.
  • Open identity The princes who pronounce the 'Fürstenspruch' are not named; local group IRI urn:regesta:actor:22706:local:fuersten, with nameInSource 'Fürstenspruch' as the only wording.
