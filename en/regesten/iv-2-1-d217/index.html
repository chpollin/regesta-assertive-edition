Hofmeister, a. a. O. 130.

Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The assurance is rf:partOf the Communication. rf:partOf is meant for provisions of a main act; using the letter as main act is defensible. The Latin clause also says the people were under his protection, a state the extractor leaves inside the evidence, which is appropriate for a narrative quotation.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No sender role for rv:Communication; the issuer of the letter is given rv:Grantor (Aussteller)
  • Gap in the model, extraction The quoted Latin 'homines, qui erant sub eius tutella et defensione' could support an existing protection state, which was not extracted to avoid inferring a state from an assurance
