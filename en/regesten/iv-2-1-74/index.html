Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding König Konrad (III.) is named in the ground of the fine and has a register candidate in this packet (RI004-002-K000316), but he appears only inside the rf:ground literal and not as a reference. The contract has no role for the party against whom the contumacia was directed; rv:Participant would be the closest fit.
  • Finding The citizens carry rv:Obligor, a role defined for an existing duty. For a fine imposed as a penalty this is defensible but not exact; the vocabulary has no role for the party sanctioned by an act.
  • Finding The second selector "Friedrich belegt" is used to anchor the grantor of the confirmation. It is a substring of the regest and Friedrich is the grammatical subject of both clauses, so the role is supported, but the selector quotes the verb of the other act.
  • Adjudication, recorded as a gap in the model In 'wegen ihrer Aufsässigkeit (contumacia) gegen König Konrad (III.)' Konrad is the target of the offence, not a party to the fine; the vocabulary has no role for it, and rv:Participant would misstate him. The wording stays in rf:ground.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No act type for imposing a penalty or fine; recorded as rv:Enactment with the citizens as rv:Obligor and 'mit einer Geldbuße' as qualifier, since no amount is stated. Payment is not asserted.
  • Gap in the model, extraction König Konrad (III.) appears only inside the ground literal and has no reference.
  • Gap in the model, review No role for the party against whom an offence was committed or who is sanctioned by a penalty.
  • Gap in the model, review A confirmation in an office (bestätigt Hermann als Bischof) has no right or office node to point rf:concerns at; the office survives only in rf:qualifier.
