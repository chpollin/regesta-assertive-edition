TEI Turtle JSON

Friedrich bestätigt dem Erzbischof Raimund von Arles die Stadt Arles, die Regalien des Erzbistums, Zölle, Weggelder, Gerichtsbarkeitsrechte (regalia, tolonea [!], pedatica, iusticias), die Verleihungen Ludwigs (des Blinden) an Erzbischof Manasse, nämlich die Hafenanlagen sowohl der Griechen als auch anderer Völker (tam portus Grecorum quam aliarum gentium ad civitatem tuam venientium), alle Wasser- und Weiderechte auf der Plaine de la Crau (de lapidoso agro, qui vulgari tuo Crau nuncupatur) und die Kastelle Fos-sur-Mer (Fossis), Albaron, Vernègues (Aluernis), Alleins (Aualone), Mondragon (Monte Draconis) und Salon. - Arbertus not. regis ; unter Verwendung der DDK. III. 132 (VU. I) und 145 (VU. II) vielleicht von Wibald verfaßt und wohl von einem Empfängerschreiber mundiert. Imperialem munificentiam decet .

Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The confirmation concerns the harbour installations, the Crau rights and the six castles, but its only selector ends at 'an Erzbischof Manasse'. The objects of the confirmation from 'nämlich' onwards rest on a passage the confirmation's evidence does not contain, so the selector does not support the whole factoid.
  • Finding Reading the castles as part of Ludwig's grants follows the 'nämlich' enumeration and is grammatically supported; the regest does not exclude that the castles were confirmed independently. The reading is defensible but should be checked by the expert against the charter.
  • Adjudication, corrected The confirmed objects are enumerated after 'nämlich' ('nämlich die Hafenanlagen sowohl der Griechen ... und Salon'), and the confirmation's rf:concerns points to all of them; the guidelines require the evidence to support the whole factoid, so the enumeration needs its own selector.
  • Correction Added a second rf:evidence selector to <urn:regesta:22813:confirmation> carrying the enumeration 'nämlich die Hafenanlagen ... und Salon'; producer of the confirmation set to correction-g10.
  • Correction <urn:regesta:22813:grant-ludwig> unchanged.
  • Gap in the model, extraction Scope of 'nämlich' unresolved: the castles may belong to Ludwig's grants to Manasse or be a separately confirmed item; they are attached to both the confirmation and the earlier grant.
  • Gap in the model, extraction 'die Verleihungen Ludwigs' (plural) recorded as one grant factoid; no way to split into individual grants from the text.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No right type for 'Regalien' as a bundle; typed with the top concept rv:LordshipRights. 'Weggelder' typed rv:Toll, a reading.
  • Gap in the model, extraction The city of Arles has no register candidate as a place (A001161 is the archbishopric); recorded as a local E53_Place.
  • Gap in the model, review The Latin right terms (regalia, tolonea [!], pedatica, iusticias) have no property; only the German labels are kept.
  • Gap in the model, review 'Regalien' has no specific right type; the top concept rv:LordshipRights is used as nearest fit.
