TEI Turtle JSON

Friedrich verbrieft Papst Eugen (III.) den Wortlaut des von den beiderseitigen Unterhändlern formulierten Vertrages (Reg. 164), nachdem die noch ausständigen Rechtshandlungen, nämlich die Eidesleistung eines Reichsministerialen in anima regis und die feierliche Zusage des Königs in die Hand des päpstlichen Legaten vollzogen worden sind. Z.: Erzbischof Arnold von Köln, die Bischöfe Hermann von Konstanz, Anselm von Havelberg, Ardicio von Como, (Adelgoz) von Chur, Abt Wibald von Stablo, Dekan Albert von Köln, Magister Notar Heinrich, der königliche Kapellan Gottfried von Viterbo; von den Laien: Herzog Welf, Markgraf Hermann von Baden, die Grafen Ulrich und Werner von Lenzburg, der Kämmerer und Reichsministeriale Anselm. - Arnoldus canc. ; möglicherweise von A II. C geschrieben und wohl unter seiner und Wibalds Beteiligung verfaßt. Regie maiestatis dignitas .

Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The confirmation points with rf:refersToAct to the oath and the promise as if it confirmed them. The regest gives them as preconditions completed before the issuance ('nachdem ... vollzogen worden sind'); the object of the issuance is the treaty text only. The temporal precondition is thereby turned into an object of confirmation.
  • Finding The treaty factoid names only the negotiators as one functional actor. 'beiderseitigen' marks two sides, Friedrich and Pope Eugen, who are the parties of the treaty; neither is referenced, so the earlier act is detached from its parties.
  • Finding A formulated treaty text is more than a negotiation, while rv:Negotiation explicitly denies a conclusion. The vocabulary has no type for an agreement or treaty; Negotiation is the most cautious available choice.
  • Finding Friedrich carries rv:Grantor in an oath sworn by a ministerial on the king's soul. The king is the principal bound by the oath, not a grantor; the vocabulary offers no principal role.
  • Adjudication, corrected The object of 'Friedrich verbrieft Papst Eugen (III.) den Wortlaut des ... Vertrages' is the treaty text only; the oath and the promise stand in the temporal clause 'nachdem die noch ausständigen Rechtshandlungen ... vollzogen worden sind'. rf:refersToAct to them reads as if the charter confirmed them. Their temporal relation is already kept by rf:qualifier "nachdem" on both factoids, so no new literal is needed.
  • Adjudication, corrected conservatively 'des von den beiderseitigen Unterhändlern formulierten Vertrages' marks two sides, and the same sentence names the two parties between whom the charter passes ('Friedrich verbrieft Papst Eugen (III.)'). Their exact function in the treaty is not stated, so only rv:Participant is safe; rv:Negotiator stays with the negotiators.
  • Adjudication, recorded as a gap in the model The vocabulary has no act type for an agreement or treaty; rv:Negotiation is the most cautious available type for 'formulierten Vertrages'. No correction.
  • Adjudication, corrected conservatively In 'die Eidesleistung eines Reichsministerialen in anima regis' the king neither transfers, confirms, grants nor enacts, so rv:Grantor misstates his part. rv:Obligor would add the legal reading that the oath binds the king; the safe fix is rv:Participant, and the principal role is recorded as a model gap.
