Transmission Deperditum; erwähnt in der Urkunde Herzog Welfs (VI.) von (1152) Juli 25, Ulm, ed. Thurgauisches UB. 2, 119 n 35; Feldmann, Welf VI. Reg. 35; Stumpf 3634a.

Commentary Aus dieser Urkunde erfahren wir den genauen Termin des Ulmer Hoftages vom Sommer 1152. Die Zeugen der Urkunde Welfs gehören zum Kreis der damals Anwesenden (Z.: aus der familia des Herzogs Welf Friedrich, sein Bruder Dieto und Hermann von der Burg Ravensburg, Konrad von Schmalneck, Friedrich von Waldburg, Adelbero von Neuburg und sein Bruder Kiso, Heinrich von Ramingen, weiters Graf Konrad von Vallei, Werner von Altdorf, Adelgoz von Immelstetten, Rudolf von Kicklingen, Heinrich von Hagnau).

Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The donation by Welf has only a Grantor reference. The passage confirms to the Stift the donation of the estate and the advocacy, so the Stift is the recipient of the earlier donation as the regest presents it.
  • Finding The confirmation's evidence is the whole sentence rather than the shortest supporting passage; the basis 'gemäß einer Urkunde' is not recorded separately.
  • Adjudication, kept The passage names the Stift as recipient of the confirmation ('bestätigt ... dem Augustiner Chorherrenstift ... die von diesem vollzogene Schenkung'). It does not say to whom Welf gave the estate and the advocacy. The extractor deliberately did not infer the donee and reported this. The same holds for Burchard's donation in 22740.
  • Adjudication, not required The factoid's roles and objects (Friedrich, the Stift, Welf's donation, estate and advocacy) are spread across almost the whole sentence. A shorter selector would drop part of the support, and the contract has no property for 'gemäß einer Urkunde'.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No property for the documentary basis of a confirmation: 'gemäß einer Urkunde Herzog Welfs (VI.)' stays only in the evidence.
  • Gap in the model, extraction Recipient of the earlier donation is not stated explicitly; the Stift is recorded only as recipient of the confirmation, not inferred as donee.
  • Open identity Herzog Welfs (VI.): no matching register candidate in this packet (only ministeriales of Welf VI appear), given a local IRI.
