Commentary Der Hinweis auf diese Erwähnung des jungen Friedrich Barbarossa am Hofe seines Onkels Konrad ist Freed, Frederick Barbarossa, 41, zu verdanken, wobei jedoch irrtümlich davon gesprochen wird, Konrad III. habe Friedrich auf Betreiben von dessen Vater ermahnt, sich nicht in die Übertragung dieser Besitzungen an das neue Prämonstratenserstift einzumengen. Es lässt sich nicht entscheiden, wer mit den mariti der beiden Markgräfinnen gemeint ist: Beatrix war in erster Ehe mit Bonifaz von Canossa (gest. 1052), in zweiter Ehe ab 1054 mit Herzog Gottfried von Niederlothringen verheiratet; Beatrix’ Tochter Mathilde ehelichte 1069/70 den Sohn ihres Stiefvaters, Herzog Gottfried den Bucklingen von Niederlothringen, und nach dessen Tod (1076) um 1090 Welf V., den Sohn Herzog Welfs IV. von Bayern.

Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The regest states a marital relation between the margravines and Gottfried and Bonifaz ('ihre beiden Ehemänner'), which is in scope as kinship. No rv:Marriage factoid records it. The pairing of each margravine with one husband is not stated, so a pairwise marriage would be inference; a candidate could still record the stated relation with the exact word 'Ehemänner' in rf:qualifier, or the gap should have been reported.
  • Finding Friedrich (Graf) and Propst Anselm act 'Auf Betreiben' (admonente), the duke 'auf Bitten'. Both are mapped to rv:Petitioner, which flattens the regest's distinction between instigation and petition. The vocabulary offers no instigator role.
  • Finding The regest marks Anselm's attribution to Arnstein with a question mark, and the Latin 'preposito prefatę ęcclesię' may point to the provost of the recipient church. The identification with the register candidate is admissible because the candidate carries the same doubt, but it remains uncertain.
  • Adjudication, recorded as a gap in the model The passage 'die von den Markgräfinnen Beatrix und Mathilde von Tuszien für das Seelenheil ihre beiden Ehemänner Gottfried (...) und Bonifaz’ (...)' leaves open which margravine was married to whom, and even which Gottfried is meant. One rv:Marriage factoid with four rv:Spouse references would assert a collective marriage, and pairwise factoids would be inferred genealogy, which the guidelines forbid. The contract cannot express an unpaired collective marital statement; the wording survives in rf:ground of urn:regesta:220518:earlier-transfer.
  • Adjudication, kept rv:Petitioner ('Auf wessen Bitten ein Akt geschieht') is the closest role for both instigation and petition, and the vocabulary has no instigator role. A factoid-level rf:qualifier "Auf Betreiben" could not say which of the three petitioners it applies to; the second evidence selector already carries the wording 'Auf Betreiben Friedrichs ...'. The missing instigator role stays a gap for the next model version.
  • Adjudication, kept The regest reads 'des Propstes Anselm (von Arnstein?)' and the packet candidate is 'Anselm ... Propst des Prämonstratenserstiftes Arnstein (?)'; name and title match, and the doubt is the register's own. The identification stays, flagged for expert review.
  • Gap in the model, extraction Marriages of Beatrix and Mathilde with Gottfried and Bonifaz not recorded: 'ihre beiden Ehemänner' does not state which husband belongs to which margravine, and a pairing would be inference.
  • Gap in the model, extraction 'Auf Betreiben' (instigation) mapped to rv:Petitioner; the vocabulary has no instigator role distinct from petitioner.
  • Gap in the model, extraction The recipient of the margravines' earlier transfer is not stated; no recipient reference recorded.
  • Gap in the model, extraction Restoration of the monastery with Premonstratensians from Arnstein not recorded; no act type and no stated legal or economic relation.
  • Gap in the model, review No instigator role distinct from petitioner ('Auf Betreiben' versus 'auf Bitten').
  • Gap in the model, review No way to record a collective marital statement whose pairing of spouses the regest leaves open.
