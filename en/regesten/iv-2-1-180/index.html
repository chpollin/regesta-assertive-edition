RI IV,2,1 n. 1801153 Juni 14Worms

TEI Turtle JSON

Friedrich verbrieft dem Erzbischof Arnold II. von Köln das unter Konrad III. dort (1151) gefällte und von ihm 1152 zu Dortmund (in burgo Tremonia, vgl. Reg. 79) gebilligte Urteil über die Unveräußerlichkeit der erzbischöflichen Tafelgüter (quatenus omnia bona, que Fredericus quondam Coloniensis archiepiscopus ... inbeneficiavit, ad potestatem archiepiscopi redire debere nec tales donationes, que contra pacem regni facte dinoscuntur, aliquam vim iuris habere) durch einen nach dem Zeugnis des Grafen Gottfried von Arnsberg und Markwards von Grumbach von Herzog Heinrich von Bayern formulierten Fürstenspruch, bestätigt weiters die vollzogenen Wiedererwerbungen, wie Wiedereinziehung des Zolls und andere Gefälle in Köln (teloneum urbis Colonie et multa episcopalia servicia), Kauf des Kastells Odenkirchen (Vdenkirken) und in Westfalen der Höfe in Homede, Anröchte (Anervst), Prummern (Brumeram), Segeroth (Segerode), Vuermůnte, Wintzen (Winisen) sowie der Güter der Ministerialen in Gevelsdorf (Geuersdorp). Z.: die Bischöfe Konrad von Worms, Gunther von Speyer, Burchard von Straßburg, Konrad von Augsburg, Dompropst Walter von Köln, Dekan Albert (von Köln), Propst Diepold von Xanten, Abt Nikolaus von Siegburg (Sigeberc), die Pröpste Arnold vom Andreasstift (in Köln), Ulrich von Soest (Sůsaz), die Herzoge Heinrich von Bayern, Heinrich von Sachsen und viele Fürsten; von den Kölner Hintersassen (De familia beati Petri): Vogt Hermann (von Eppendorf), Heinrich von Volmarstein (Uolmudestein), Heinrich von Alpen (Alpheim), Truchseß Adolf, Schenk Randolf, Raboto von Odenkirchen, Amalrich von Wormersdorf (Wormisdorp). - Arnoldus canc. ; außerhalb der Kanzlei verfaßt und von zwei Kölner Schreibern mundiert, diktatgleich mit DF. I. 60 (Reg. 181); SI. 1. Quoniam ordinatione excellentissimę .

Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The 'durch'-clause can attach to the 1151 judgment or to Friedrich's present 'verbrieft'. Folding it into the 1151 judgment makes Herzog Heinrich von Bayern a judge and Gottfried of Arnsberg and Markward of Grumbach witnesses of an act dated by the qualifier '(1151)', which the passage does not establish. The two readings should not be collapsed into one dated factoid.
  • Finding The judgment's content is not modelled: goods that the former archbishop Friedrich enfeoffed shall return to the archbishop, and such donations made against the peace of the realm have no legal force. The contract could express this as provisions of the judgment (rv:Revocation or rv:Enactment, rf:partOf the judgment) and the earlier enfeoffment by the former archbishop, with 'contra pacem regni' as rf:ground.
  • Finding 'unter Konrad III. gefällt' places the judgment under Konrad's reign or presidency; rv:Judge is a stronger claim than the wording carries.
  • Finding 'Wiedereinziehung' is a recovery by the church, typed rv:Restitution (Rückgabe). Acceptable, though the actor who recovers is not named and no restitution by another party is stated.
  • Adjudication, corrected conservatively In 'Friedrich verbrieft ... das unter Konrad III. dort (1151) gefällte und von ihm 1152 zu Dortmund ... gebilligte Urteil ... durch einen ... formulierten Fürstenspruch' the clause can attach to the earlier judgment or to the present 'verbrieft'; the extractor itself flags the ambiguity. Attaching it to the dated 1151 factoid is temporal overreach. The safe fix gives the Fürstenspruch its own undated factoid and leaves its attachment open; the witnesses testify in the narrated proceedings, so rv:Witness is admissible, while the formulating duke gets rv:Participant as Bishop Konrad of Augsburg does for the parallel wording in 22800.
  • Adjudication, corrected conservatively The parenthesis 'quatenus omnia bona, que Fredericus quondam Coloniensis archiepiscopus ... inbeneficiavit, ad potestatem archiepiscopi redire debere nec tales donationes, que contra pacem regni facte dinoscuntur, aliquam vim iuris habere' is the substance of the judgment, in scope and expressible as a provision of the judgment plus the earlier enfeoffment it refers to. 'Fredericus quondam Coloniensis archiepiscopus' matches the packet candidate RI004-002-F000654 ('Erzbischof von Köln') by name and title. Typing the provision as rv:Revocation would read the judgment as an act of revocation; rv:Enactment ('eine rechtliche Festlegung') is safer. The relative clause 'que contra pacem regni facte dinoscuntur' restricts which donations are void, so it goes to rf:condition rather than rf:ground.
  • Adjudication, corrected conservatively 'das unter Konrad III. dort (1151) gefällte' places the judgment under his reign or presidency but does not state that he pronounced it; rv:Judge claims more than the wording carries. rv:Participant with the exact phrase keeps the reading open.
