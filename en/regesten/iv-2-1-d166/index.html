Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The representatives are typed crm:E74_Group but use the bare register IRI. The guidelines reserve the bare register IRI for crm:E21_Person and require the ':community' suffix for a group. The same register ID may be used as a person IRI in other files, which would give one IRI two classes across the run.
  • Finding The county is modelled as crm:E53_Place here and in 22792, but as crm:E30_Right (LordshipRights) in 22800. Both readings are defensible; the inconsistency hinders querying the Chiavenna dispute across records.
  • Adjudication, corrected The guidelines (Actors and identity) reserve the bare register IRI for crm:E21_Person and give a community the IRI urn:regesta:actor:REGISTER_ID:community typed crm:E74_Group. The extractor's reading of 'die Vertreter von Chiavenna' as a collective is defensible, so the type stays and the IRI is brought into the convention.
  • Adjudication, not required The contract allows both a right and a place as rf:concerns targets and gives no rule for a Grafschaft; each file is valid for its own passage ('Zugehörigkeit der Grafschaft Chiavenna' versus 'den Streit ... um die Grafschaft Chiavenna'). A cross-record alignment is a modelling decision for the expert, not a correction under this contract.
