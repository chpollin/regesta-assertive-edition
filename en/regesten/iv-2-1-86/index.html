Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The pope approbates 'diese Wahl', Friedrich's election, which the passage reports as an earlier act ('die Botschaft von seiner Wahl'). The guidelines require the earlier act as its own act factoid joined by rf:refersToAct from the confirmation. The candidate folds the election into the second evidence selector of the approbation and gives the approbation Friedrich as Recipient, so the earlier act is collapsed into its confirmation.
  • Finding The announcement of a legate is omitted. It is an act of the letter that the contract can express as a communication part of the letter; modelling it as rv:Mission would overreach, since only the announcement is stated.
  • Finding The pope reports having heard good things about Friedrich from cardinal priest Oktavian. Oktavian has a register candidate in this packet (RI004-002-O000158) and is not referenced at all. The report is a communication from Oktavian to the pope that the contract can express.
  • Finding 'in Aussicht stellen' is weaker than a Zusicherung. The qualifier 'in Aussicht' preserves the weakening, so the factoid does not overstate; the coronation itself is not given as rf:concerns.
  • Finding The pope as writer is rv:Participant because the role scheme has no sender role. Consistent with the vocabulary, but the author of a communication is not distinguishable from other participants.
  • Adjudication, corrected The passage reports the election as an earlier act ('die Botschaft von seiner Wahl') and then 'approbiert diese Wahl ausdrücklich'. The guidelines require the earlier act as its own act factoid joined by rf:refersToAct from the confirmation; rv:Appointment is the type the run uses for an election (cf. 22730).
  • Adjudication, corrected conservatively 'kündigt die baldige Entsendung eines Legaten an' is a distinct act of the letter and in scope, but only the announcement is stated; an rv:Mission would assert a sending the passage does not report. The letter's other contents are already modelled as rv:Communication parts of the letter, so the same weak form is safe.
  • Adjudication, not required 'als er schon von Kardinalpriester O(ktavian) Gutes über ihn gehört habe' is a subordinate clause explaining the pope's joy, not a dispositive act or a kinship, economic or legal relation; adding it would widen the scope beyond central passages.
  • Adjudication, not required The guidelines reserve rf:concerns for a right, thing, place or community; a coronation is none of these, and the qualifier 'in Aussicht' with the evidence already carries the wording.
  • Correction Added <urn:regesta:22713:election> (rf:ActFactoid, rv:Appointment) with evidence "die Botschaft von seiner Wahl", reference RI004-002-F000471 rv:Recipient nameInSource "seiner", rf:qualifier "Wahl"; no electors, no date, no rf:partOf.
  • Correction Added rf:refersToAct <urn:regesta:22713:election> to <urn:regesta:22713:approbation>; approbation otherwise unchanged and now attributed to correction-g5.
  • Correction Added <urn:regesta:22713:legate-announcement> (rf:ActFactoid, rv:Communication) with evidence "kündigt die baldige Entsendung eines Legaten an" and "Papst Eugen (III.) schreibt an König F(riedrich)", references E000589 rv:Participant and F000471 rv:Addressee, rf:qualifier "baldige", rf:partOf <urn:regesta:22713:letter>. No rv:Mission, no legate actor, no outcome.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No sender or author role for rv:Communication; the pope is recorded as rv:Participant.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No act type for a royal election; the election approved by the pope and Friedrich's message about it are not modelled as acts, and the approbation has no concerned object.
  • Gap in the model, extraction The announced dispatch of a legate is an announcement of a future mission without an act type of its own; not extracted.
  • Gap in the model, extraction 'stellt ihm die Kaiserkrönung in Aussicht' typed rv:Assurance with qualifier 'in Aussicht'; whether a prospect counts as an assurance needs expert review.
  • Gap in the model, review No sender or author role for rv:Communication.
  • Gap in the model, review No act type for a coronation or a coronation oath; the oath is approximated as rv:Assurance.
  • Open identity 'seines Vorgängers' is a regest-local functional actor; no register candidate in this packet names the predecessor.
  • Open identity 'seines Krönungseides' read as Friedrich's own coronation oath; it could also be read as the predecessor's.
