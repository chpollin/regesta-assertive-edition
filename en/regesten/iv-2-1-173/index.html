TEI Turtle JSON

Friedrich entscheidet den Streit zwischen dem Bischof Ardicio von Como und den Vertretern der Leute von Chiavenna (Guibertus vero Crassus et Guibertus Porcus) um die Grafschaft Chiavenna zu Ostern (April 19) in der Bamberger Pfalz nach einer in Konstanz vertagten Verhandlung (Regg. 165 und 166) entgegen seiner im Vorjahr zu Ulm getroffenen Verfügung (Regg. 117 und 118) nach Vorweisung und Vorlesung der Urkunden des Bischofs von Como und Ablehnung der Chiavennater Urkunden sowie ihrer Behauptung, die Grafschaft gehöre zum Herzogtum Schwaben (Verum quia neque de manu patris nostri Friderici ducis Sueuorum neque de manu nostra, dum adhuc eiusdem ducatus dignitate fungeremur, aliquam investituram eos seu privilegium suscepisse cognovimus, set pocius, postquam regni solium divina dispositione conscendimus, privilegium de ipso comitatu a manu nostra de respectu regni, sicut supra notavimus, susceperunt), durch einen von Bischof Konrad von Augsburg formulierten Fürstenspruch zugunsten von Como. Z.: die päpstlichen Legaten Kardinalpriester Bernhard und Kardinaldiakon Gregor, die Bischöfe Anselm von Havelberg, Otto von Freising, Wichmann von Zeitz, Gebhard von Würzburg und Konrad von Augsburg, die Markgrafen Albrecht (Adalbert) von Sachsen, Otakar von Steier, die Grafen Rudolf von Ramsberg, Friedrich von Beichlingen, Markward von Grumbach; von den Leuten von Como waren anwesend: Iohannes Calignus, der Richter Albert, der Peregrin genannt wird, Iohannes Sescalcus, Aliprandus de Plazola, Arnaldus de Ripa und Martinus de Castello, ein Verwandter (nepos) des Bischofs (Ardicio); als Vertreter Chiavennas waren anwesend: die zwei erwähnten Wiberti (Guibertus vero Crassus et Guibertus Porcus), Manfredus de Landran, Soldanus, Karolus und der Priester Marchisius de Balerna . - Arnoldus canc. vice Maguntini archiep. et archicanc. ; verfaßt und wohl auch geschrieben von A II. C; das Original war mit einem SI. versehen. Imperii nostri nos .

Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding 'zugunsten von Como' is modelled as a regest-local group labelled 'Como'. In this regest Como stands for the episcopal side of the dispute (Bishop Ardicio and his church), not for a community of Como; a bare group 'Como' invites reading it as the commune. The party that wins, Bishop Ardicio, is not referenced in the judgment at all.
  • Finding The judgment points to the Ulm disposition with rf:refersToAct and keeps 'entgegen' only as a free qualifier. The regest says the judgment goes against that earlier disposition; refersToAct, defined for confirmations and assurances, reads as if the judgment built on it. The opposition is lost as a relation.
  • Finding The recipient reference has no rf:nameInSource; the Latin names the recipients as 'eos'. Allowed by the shapes, but the identification of 'eos' with the people of Chiavenna is an unmarked reading.
  • Finding The county is a crm:E30_Right here and a crm:E53_Place in 22792 and 22793.
  • Adjudication, not required The contract allows both a right and a place as rf:concerns targets and gives no rule for a Grafschaft; each file is valid for its own passage ('Zugehörigkeit der Grafschaft Chiavenna' versus 'den Streit ... um die Grafschaft Chiavenna'). A cross-record alignment is a modelling decision for the expert, not a correction under this contract.
  • Adjudication, kept The passage says 'zugunsten von Como', not 'zugunsten des Bischofs'. A regest-local actor labelled with the source wording asserts no identity with the commune or the bishop, as the guidelines require for an actor without a matching candidate; the packet of 22800 holds no candidate for the church or commune of Como. Identifying 'Como' with Bishop Ardicio would be an interpretive step. Ardicio stays referenced on the dispute, to which the judgment links.
  • Adjudication, corrected conservatively rf:refersToAct only states that the later act refers to an earlier act of the same regest; it remains true for a judgment given 'entgegen seiner im Vorjahr zu Ulm getroffenen Verfügung' and keeps the link. The defect is the detached literal 'entgegen', which does not say against what. The safe fix keeps the link and replaces the bare word with the exact phrase.
  • Adjudication, not required rf:nameInSource is optional in rf:ReferenceShape (maxCount 1, no minCount). 'eos' stands in the preceding negated clause, not in the evidence passage of this factoid ('... susceperunt'), so adding it would tie the name to a different passage.
  • Correction urn:regesta:22800:judgment: rf:qualifier "entgegen" replaced with "entgegen seiner im Vorjahr zu Ulm getroffenen Verfügung"; "zu Ostern (April 19)" and all three rf:refersToAct values kept; factoid attributed to the corrector.
  • Gap in the model, extraction Claimant and respondent are not distinguishable from the regest; both dispute parties are rv:Participant.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No role for the formulator of a Fürstenspruch; Bischof Konrad von Augsburg is rv:Participant and the deciding princes are not modelled.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No property for a judgment overriding an earlier disposition; the Ulm disposition is linked by rf:refersToAct with the qualifier 'entgegen'.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No property for the place of an act (Konstanz, Ulm, Bamberger Pfalz).
  • Gap in the model, extraction The negative statement that the Chiavennese received no investiture from the duke or from Friedrich as duke cannot be represented and was not recorded.
  • Gap in the model, extraction The kinship 'ein Verwandter (nepos) des Bischofs (Ardicio)' of Martinus de Castello stands inside the Z.: list and was skipped under the witness-list rule; a reviewer may judge it in scope.
  • Gap in the model, review No relation for an act contrary to or overriding an earlier act ('entgegen').
  • Gap in the model, review Negated acts cannot be expressed: the Latin states that the Chiavennese received no investiture from Duke Friedrich or from Friedrich as duke.
  • Gap in the model, review No property for places of acts (Bamberger Pfalz, Konstanz, Ulm).
  • Gap in the model, review The witness list carries a kinship statement (Martinus de Castello, nepos of the bishop) and the list of representatives present; the guidelines exclude witness lists wholesale, so these are not extractable.
  • Open identity 'Leute von Chiavenna' is a regest-local group without register candidate; it is the implied recipient of the royal privilege ('susceperunt'), referenced without nameInSource.
