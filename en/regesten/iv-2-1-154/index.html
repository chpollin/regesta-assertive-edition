Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The regest states that Duke Matthäus of Lorraine had wrongfully usurped the castle. The candidate omits this clause and the actor, citing the lack of a type for wrongful seizure. The contract could express it as a StateFactoid rv:Possession (defined neutrally as possession 'wie das Regest es sagt') with Matthäus as rv:Possessor and the exact wording as qualifier. Matthäus matches the packet candidate RI004-002-M000670 by name and title.
  • Finding rv:Grant presupposes a transferring actor; the passage names none and speaks of acquisition with the ruler's help. The extractor reports this as a model gap, which is accurate.
  • Adjudication, corrected conservatively 'die Burg Homburg, die Herzog Matthäus von Lothringen unrechtmäßig usurpiert hat' is central and in scope (a legal relation to a castle). The act type for usurpation is missing, but the resulting holding is expressible as rv:Possession, defined 'wie das Regest es sagt', with the exact wording 'unrechtmäßig usurpiert' as qualifier so that no lawful possession is asserted. 'Herzog Matthäus von Lothringen' matches the packet candidate RI004-002-M000670 'Matthäus // Herzog von (Ober-)Lothringen' by name and title.
