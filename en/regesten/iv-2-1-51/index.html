Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The evidence ends before 'bis er den König gesprochen habe', which bounds the requested postponement. The limit belongs to the content of the request and is neither in the evidence nor in an exact-wording literal.
  • Finding The kinship is attached to Ludwig through 'seines'. The more natural reading of the sentence supports Ludwig, and the file flags the alternative reading (Friedrich) in a comment. Acceptable, but the uncertainty lives only in a Turtle comment, not in the data.
  • Finding 'beim König' (candidate K000316) is the target of the requested intercession and the one who decides; the vocabulary has no role for the target of an intercession, and making him an addressee would be wrong, so leaving him unreferenced is correct under the contract.
  • Adjudication, corrected The request reads 'oder wenigstens eine Verschiebung der Entscheidung bewirken, bis er den König gesprochen habe'; the evidence stops at 'bewirken', so the bound of the alternative request is in neither the evidence nor a literal. It is a temporal qualification in exact wording, which rf:qualifier carries.
  • Adjudication, recorded as a gap in the model 'er möge sich beim König ... einsetzen': the king is neither addressee nor beneficiary of the request, and the vocabulary has no role for the target of an intercession. Leaving him unreferenced is correct.
  • Correction In <urn:regesta:22678:request> extended the oa:exact of the evidence selector to end with ', bis er den König gesprochen habe' (now the whole of summary_plain without the final period).
  • Correction Added rf:qualifier 'bis er den König gesprochen habe' beside 'brieflich'.
  • Correction Attributed <urn:regesta:22678:request> to correction-g3 as a changed factoid; the kinship factoid keeps extraction-batch-13.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No role for the party at whom a requested intercession aims (the king); he carries no reference.
  • Gap in the model, extraction The requested outcomes (Erhebung auf den Würzburger Bischofsstuhl, Verschiebung der Entscheidung) have no slot other than the evidence passage; rf:concerns has no fitting class for a bishop's see.
  • Gap in the model, review No role for the target of a requested intercession (the king).
  • Gap in the model, review No way to link the requested elevation as an intended, unperformed rv:Appointment with Gebhard and the Würzburg see.
  • Open identity "seines Verwandten Gebhard": the possessive is read as Landgraf Ludwig; grammatically it could also refer to Herzog Friedrich, the subject of "er möge".
  • Open identity "beim König" names the king by title only; the packet candidate RI004-002-K000316 (Konrad III.) was not assigned because the passage gives no name and the king carries no reference.
