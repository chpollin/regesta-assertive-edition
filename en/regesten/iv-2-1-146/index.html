Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding rf:refersToAct is defined for a confirmation or assurance referring to an earlier act. The enfeoffment follows the judgment ('nachdem') but does not confirm it. The 'erneut' implies an earlier enfeoffment that the regest does not report as an act, so the qualifier alone is adequate.
  • Finding The register entry denotes the monks of Niederaltaich as a body, while the passage speaks of 'einiger Mönche', a subset. Using the community IRI slightly widens the actor.
  • Finding Berthold von Bogen is the advocate through whom the induction is to be carried out; rv:Participant is acceptable but the vocabulary has no executor role.
  • Adjudication, kept rf:refersToAct is defined for 'a later act such as a confirmation or assurance', so the list is not exclusive. The regest ties the renewed enfeoffment to the judgment ('belehnt ... erneut ..., nachdem ... ein diesbezüglicher Spruch gefällt worden ist'); a reference from the later act to the earlier one the same regest reports is defensible and implies no date.
  • Adjudication, kept N000177 is the packet's own register entry 'Niederaltaich ... // Mönche', matching the passage by name; the label 'einiger Mönche' and rf:nameInSource keep the restriction in the source wording. A regest-local group would discard a valid packet identification.
  • Gap in the model, extraction The monks' Unmutsäußerungen are typed rv:Dispute with role Participant, since the regest names neither a formal claim nor claimant/respondent roles
  • Gap in the model, extraction No role for the executor of an induction into possession (Klostervogt Berthold), recorded as Participant
  • Gap in the model, review No role for the executor of an order (the person through whom an act is to be carried out).
  • Open identity einiger Mönche: identified with the register entry for the Niederaltaich monks as a community IRI, although the passage speaks of some monks only
  • Open identity der anwesenden Reichsfürsten: no register candidate, regest-local group
