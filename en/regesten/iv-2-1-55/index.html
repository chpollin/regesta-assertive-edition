Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The regest reports a peace mediated with stated terms, not merely a negotiation. rv:Negotiation is defined as not establishing a conclusion, so the factoid loses that a peace was reached, and the provisions then hang off an act that by definition has no outcome. rv:Enactment (a legal settlement made) would express the concluded peace within the contract; Friedrich as rv:Mediator fits either type.
  • Finding The revenue clause is attached by rf:partOf to the peace yet typed as a performed rv:Grant, while the two other terms of the same peace are rv:Enactment. Either it is a term of the peace, then it is a provision (rv:Enactment), or it is a separate grant introduced by außerdem, then rf:partOf overreaches. The candidate's own comment marks the question as unresolved, but the triples assert one reading.
  • Finding The reciprocity (dafür) between the return of the prisoners and Welf's undertaking is only implicit in the shared evidence span. The contract has no property linking two provisions as consideration.
  • Finding Mertingen is typed crm:E53_Place, while the regest has Welf receive the village together with the revenues, as an object of transfer. A crm:E18_Physical_Thing for the village would be closer.
  • Adjudication, corrected 'Friedrich vermittelt zwischen König Konrad (III.) und Herzog Welf (VI.) einen Frieden, nach dem Welf ...' reports a peace brokered with its terms, while rv:Negotiation is defined as not establishing a conclusion, so the provisions hang off an act that by definition has no outcome. rv:Enactment, a settlement made without its observance being established, expresses it within the contract and matches the treatment of the treaty in 22668 of this group. The regest does not say that Konrad and Welf negotiated, so the parties take rv:Participant.
  • Adjudication, corrected conservatively 'außerdem erhält Welf auch Reichseinkünfte mit dem Dorf Mertingen' stands in the same present-tense series as 'nach dem Welf die Gefangenen zurückerhält', which the candidate types as a provision; 'auch' links it to the terms of the peace. The guidelines make a provision an act of enactment that does not establish its performance, so the safe reading keeps rf:partOf and types the clause as a provision, without asserting a grantor or a completed transfer.
  • Adjudication, recorded as a gap in the model 'und dafür künftig nicht mehr gegen den König kämpfen wird': the contract has no property for consideration between provisions, and rf:qualifier is for wording that qualifies type or time, not for a relation between factoids. The word stays in the evidence.
  • Adjudication, kept 'Reichseinkünfte mit dem Dorf Mertingen' can be read as the place the revenues attach to or as an estate; the regest does not decide it, and E53 with the label 'Dorf Mertingen' asserts nothing the passage does not carry.
  • Correction In <urn:regesta:22682:peace> changed rf:factoidType rv:Negotiation to rv:Enactment and added rf:qualifier 'Frieden'; changed the roles of peace-ref-konrad and peace-ref-welf from rv:Negotiator to rv:Participant; kept Friedrich as rv:Mediator, the evidence and the provisions' rf:partOf; updated the Turtle comment.
  • Correction In <urn:regesta:22682:revenues> changed rf:factoidType rv:Grant to rv:Enactment, kept rf:partOf the peace, the evidence and rf:concerns; changed revenues-ref-welf from rv:Recipient to rv:Beneficiary; rewrote the comment as a provision while keeping the separate-act reading of 'außerdem' open for expert review.
  • Correction Attributed the two changed factoids to correction-g3; prisoners and no-fighting keep extraction-batch-14.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No act type for a concluded or mediated peace settlement; the main act 'vermittelt ... einen Frieden' is typed rv:Negotiation, which understates the conclusion the regest reports.
  • Gap in the model, extraction Prisoners returned to Welf cannot be modelled as rf:concerns (persons are not E18 things); the provision carries Welf as Beneficiary only.
  • Gap in the model, extraction Unresolved reading: whether 'außerdem erhält Welf auch Reichseinkünfte mit dem Dorf Mertingen' is a term of the peace (modelled with rf:partOf) or a separate act; the grantor is not named.
  • Gap in the model, review No act type for a concluded peace or settlement.
  • Gap in the model, review No relation for consideration between provisions (one term in return for another).
