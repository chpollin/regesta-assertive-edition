Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The regest reports a letter from Welf (VI.) to Guido (III.) Guerra announcing that he will intercede with the emperor for him. rv:Communication and rv:Assurance cover this, and the packet carries matching candidates W000272 and G001188. The abstention reason ('no kinship, economic or legal relation') applies the exception clause of the military exclusion to the whole regest.
  • Adjudication, corrected conservatively The guidelines exclude only 'military movement alone, travel, and hostage taking'. 'Herzog Welf (VI.) teilt Guido (III.) Guerra, Graf von Tuszien, mit, dass er sich beim Kaiser für ihn einsetzen werde' is a letter (rv:Communication) carrying an assurance (rv:Assurance), the same kind of communication the run models in 220530. The coming with troops and the support against enemies remain out of scope as military. The fix is conservative because the vocabulary has no intercession type and no role for the person before whom one intercedes: the emperor gets no reference and the intercession stays in exact wording as rf:qualifier. Welf and Guido match the packet candidates W000272 and G001188 by name and title.
  • Correction Replaced the abstention: added actors urn:regesta:actor:RI004-002-W000272 ("Herzog Welf (VI.)") and urn:regesta:actor:RI004-002-G001188 ("Guido (III.) Guerra, Graf von Tuszien").
  • Correction Added rf:ActFactoid urn:regesta:220532:letter (rv:Communication), Welf rv:Grantor, Guido rv:Addressee; producer correction-g11.
  • Correction Added rf:ActFactoid urn:regesta:220532:assurance-intercession (rv:Assurance, rf:partOf the letter), two evidence selectors, Welf rv:Grantor, Guido rv:Beneficiary, rf:qualifier "sich beim Kaiser für ihn einsetzen werde"; producer correction-g11.
  • Gap in the model, extraction Borderline abstention: the letter promises intercession with the emperor and armed support against enemies ('Unterstützung bei der Niederwerfung ihrer Feinde'); read as political-military, not a kinship, economic or legal relation. Whether such alliance promises count as rv:Assurance in scope is an open expert question.
  • Gap in the model, review No act type for intercession on another's behalf.
