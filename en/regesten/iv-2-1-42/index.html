Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The file holds only the source document node although the regest reports a letter and a request that the contract can express. Roger (R000806) issues the letter, Welf's people carry it, Herzog Friedrich von Schwaben is its addressee, and the letter asks for support of Welf (W000272). The request establishes neither the support nor any action against Konrad.
  • Adjudication, corrected conservatively The regest states 'König Roger von Sizilien gibt den Leuten Herzog Welfs einen Brief an Herzog Friedrich von Schwaben mit, in dem dieser zur Unterstützung des Welfen gegen König Konrad III. aufgefordert wird'. This is a communication and a request, not military movement, and rv:Communication, rv:Request, rv:Envoy, rv:Addressee and rv:Beneficiary express it. Roger (R000806, König von Sizilien) and Welf (W000272, Herzog) match packet candidates by name and title. Herzog Friedrich von Schwaben has no candidate in this packet and the shared ruler label 'Friedrich Barbarossa' does not carry the ducal title, so he gets a regest-local actor. The letter's sender has no specific role, so Roger is rv:Participant, and he is not made petitioner of the request because the passage is passive. Konrad III., against whom support is requested, has no role and stays unreferenced.
  • Correction Replaced the abstention with two act factoids per adjudication-g3, keeping <urn:regesta:record:22669> a crm:E31_Document.
  • Correction Added <urn:regesta:22669:letter> (rv:Communication) with evidence 'König Roger von Sizilien gibt den Leuten Herzog Welfs einen Brief an Herzog Friedrich von Schwaben mit' and references Roger (RI004-002-R000806, rv:Participant), the local group 'den Leuten Herzog Welfs' (rv:Envoy) and the local person 'Herzog Friedrich von Schwaben' (rv:Addressee).
