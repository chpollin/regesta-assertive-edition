Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The regest says only versprochenen Geldsumme; it does not say who promised it. That the citizens made the promise is inferred from the prohibition addressed to them. The factoid also has no rv:Grantor, so the act of promising has no actor, while the citizens appear as rv:Obligor, a state role.
  • Finding The citizens are identified as the community of the place candidate Höxter. This follows the community rule, and the extractor flagged it as unresolved.
  • Adjudication, corrected conservatively 'untersagt ihnen die Entrichtung der versprochenen Geldsumme an die Genannten' states that a promised sum was due from the citizens to Folkwin and Widekind. It does not state who made the promise. An rv:Obligation state with Obligor and Creditor rests directly on the passage. ActTypeShape restricts rf:refersToAct to act factoids, so the prohibition's link to it has to go.
  • Correction Changed <urn:regesta:22750:promised-sum> from rf:ActFactoid rv:Assurance to rf:StateFactoid rv:Obligation, keeping its IRI, both evidence selectors, references ref-sum-buerger (rv:Obligor), ref-sum-folkwin and ref-sum-widekind (rv:Creditor) and rf:qualifier "versprochenen"; comment updated.
  • Correction Removed <urn:regesta:22750:prohibition-payment> rf:refersToAct <urn:regesta:22750:promised-sum>, which ActTypeShape no longer permits.
  • Correction Set prov:wasAttributedTo of promised-sum and prohibition-payment to the corrector.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No role for the wrongdoer; rv:Participant used.
  • Gap in the model, extraction The citizens' earlier promise of a sum is modelled as rv:Assurance with rv:Obligor and rv:Creditor; the vocabulary has no act type for a promise to pay. No amount is stated.
  • Gap in the model, review No role for the perpetrators of a wrong to be avenged; rv:Participant is a placeholder.
  • Open identity Bürgern von Höxter (Huxera): identified as community of the place candidate RI004-002-H001241 (urn:regesta:actor:RI004-002-H001241:community); the packet has no candidate for the citizenry itself.
