Friedrich bestätigt den Herren von Bulgaro (Reinhero de Bulgaro et filiis Philippi et Manfredi) die namentlich genannten Besitzungen und Lehen, insbesondere die Burg Crevacuore (Creuecor) und nimmt die Erwerbungen aus der Zeit Heinrichs (V.), Lothars (III.) und Konrads (III.) unter Vorbehalt der königlichen Gerechtsame in seinen Schutz. Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The protection grant names only the ruler. The acquisitions taken into protection are those of the lords of Bulgaro, who are the recipients of the whole regest; without them the factoid does not say whose acquisitions are protected.
  • Finding The Latin says 'et filiis' without a possessive; reading them as Reinher's sons is the natural reading and supported by the coordination, but it is a reading.
  • Finding The time span of the acquisitions ('aus der Zeit Heinrichs (V.), Lothars (III.) und Konrads (III.)') is kept only in the label of the thing node, not as rf:qualifier. Correctly, no date is derived from it.
  • Adjudication, corrected conservatively 'und nimmt die Erwerbungen aus der Zeit Heinrichs (V.), Lothars (III.) und Konrads (III.) ... in seinen Schutz' is coordinated with the confirmation to the lords of Bulgaro but names no protected persons, so assigning them as rv:Recipient rests on ellipsis. The safe fix is the reviewer's alternative: connect the protection as a secondary act to the confirmation, which carries the recipients.
  • Adjudication, not required The wording 'aus der Zeit Heinrichs (V.), Lothars (III.) und Konrads (III.)' is kept in the label of the thing node and the evidence, and no date is derived; the reviewer marks the addition optional.
  • Correction Added rf:partOf urn:regesta:22786:confirmation-bulgaro to urn:regesta:22786:protection-erwerbungen; no new references; exception kept; producer set to correction-g8.
  • Gap in the model, extraction 'Lehen' are confirmed without a named lord; no feudal relationship recorded.
