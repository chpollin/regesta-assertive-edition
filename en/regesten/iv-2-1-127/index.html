Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The pope as writer of his own letter and the bishops as writers of Reg. 103 are recorded as rv:Participant. This follows from the missing sender role, not from a misreading.
  • Finding The pope's statement that he is astonished at the bishops' intervention and cannot approve it is part of the letter's content and is not represented. The vocabulary offers no type for a stance or disapproval, so this is a model gap rather than an extraction error.
  • Finding A declaration that consent will never be given is typed rv:Enactment and the pope is given rv:Grantor. This is the closest available type, but the passage states a refusal, not a legal determination; the 'niemals' qualifier keeps the essential wording.
  • Adjudication, recorded as a gap in the model The vocabulary has no sender role for rv:Communication; rv:Participant is the contract's fallback. No correction.
  • Adjudication, recorded as a gap in the model 'daß er über ihr Eintreten in der Magdeburger Angelegenheit maßlos erstaunt sei, dieses nicht billigen könne' is a stance; the act scheme has no type for it and forcing it into rv:Enactment would change its meaning.
  • Adjudication, kept A binding declaration 'niemals seine Zustimmung geben werde' is the closest available type, and the qualifier 'niemals' and ground 'aus den dargelegten Gründen' keep the wording. A refusal type is a gap for the next model version.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No role for the author or sender of a letter; Papst Eugen (III.) and the senders of Reg. 103 are rv:Participant in the communication factoids.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No act type for a refusal of consent; the pope's declaration that he will never consent to Wichmann's translation is an rv:Enactment partOf the letter, with 'niemals' as qualifier.
  • Gap in the model, extraction The pope's disapproval of the bishops' intervention ('maßlos erstaunt', 'nicht billigen könne') has no fitting act type and is covered only as evidence of the earlier letter's subject.
  • Gap in the model, review No sender or author role for a Communication.
  • Gap in the model, review No act type for a refusal of consent or a stated disapproval.
  • Open identity Eberhard von Salzburg, Hartwig von Bremen and Hillin von Trier have no matching register candidate in this packet and are regest-local actors.
  • Open identity The authors of Reg. 103 are taken from 'deren', which refers back to all eleven addressees.
