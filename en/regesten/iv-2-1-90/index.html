RI IV,2,1 n. 90(1152 Mai)

TEI Turtle JSON

Wibald schreibt an Erzbischof Arnold von Köln unter anderem, daß ihm der König wegen seiner Verdienste um seine Wahl und der späteren Leistungen sehr dankbar sei, Lothringen (regnum Lotharingiae) seiner Disposition überlasse, in welcher Hinsicht der Kanzler (Arnold) und der Notar (Heinrich) den Bemühungen einiger Laien(fürsten) beim König entgegenwirken, und daß er über die neulich, allerdings ohne Wissen des Königs eroberte Burg frei verfügen könne; den Abt von Prüm möge er schonen, damit sich der König nicht versündige (ne serenus augustus in Deum peccet cum suae famae vel modico detrimento ).

Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The final clause is a request in the letter: Arnold should spare the abbot of Prüm so that the king does not sin. The contract can express it fully (rv:Request with Wibald as petitioner, Arnold as rv:Addressee, the abbot as a local or functional actor, rf:ground in exact wording). It is a distinct act of the letter, not commentary, and it is omitted.
  • Finding The regest gives the reason for the king's gratitude and favour: Arnold's merits concerning the election and his later services. The candidate keeps the words in the evidence selector but records no rf:ground, so the reason is not a queryable part of the factoid.
  • Finding Chancellor Arnold and notary Heinrich counteract the efforts of some lay princes at court concerning Lotharingia. Both have register candidates in this packet (RI004-002-A001203, RI004-002-H000404) and are not referenced. The vocabulary has no act type for support or counteraction, so only a partial expression (rv:Dispute with participants) is possible.
  • Finding The grant is reported by Wibald, not enacted in the regested document. rf:partOf the letter is the only marker of this; the guidelines use partOf for provisions of a main act, so the reported status is implicit.
  • Adjudication, corrected 'den Abt von Prüm möge er schonen, damit sich der König nicht versündige' is a distinct request of the letter, expressible with rv:Request and rf:ground. The passage names the abbot only by office, so the register candidate Rether (RI004-002-R000500) cannot be matched by name; a regest-local actor is required.
  • Adjudication, kept In 'daß ihm der König wegen seiner Verdienste um seine Wahl und der späteren Leistungen sehr dankbar sei, Lothringen ... seiner Disposition überlasse' the reason belongs grammatically to the gratitude clause; attaching it to the disposition would assert a reason the regest does not give for that act. The wording stays in the evidence.
  • Adjudication, recorded as a gap in the model 'der Kanzler (Arnold) und der Notar (Heinrich) den Bemühungen einiger Laien(fürsten) beim König entgegenwirken' is opposition at court, for which the vocabulary has no act type; rv:Dispute would turn it into a lawsuit the passage does not report.
  • Correction Added regest-local actor <urn:regesta:actor:22717:local:abt-pruem> (crm:E21_Person, "den Abt von Prüm"); not identified with RI004-002-R000500.
  • Correction Added <urn:regesta:22717:f-request-spare-abbot> (rf:ActFactoid, rv:Request) with evidence "den Abt von Prüm möge er schonen, damit sich der König nicht versündige" and "Wibald schreibt an Erzbischof Arnold von Köln", references W000420 rv:Petitioner, A001202 rv:Addressee, abt-pruem rv:Beneficiary, rf:ground "damit sich der König nicht versündige", rf:partOf <urn:regesta:22717:f-letter>.
