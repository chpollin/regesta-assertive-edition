Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The threat of war is a second act of the same passage and carries an explicit condition. The candidate drops both. No act type fits a threat exactly, but the contract could carry it as an act with rf:condition in exact wording, or the omission should at least be reported as a model gap.
  • Finding The envoy is not acting as an envoy in this act; he is the person whose capture the complaint is about. rv:Envoy (who is sent or delivers a message) describes his general function, not his role in the complaint.
  • Finding The regest marks the sender as uncertain with a question mark. The candidate maps the mention to the shared ruler IRI without any trace of that doubt beyond the label literal, so a query over rf:actor treats the identification as certain.
  • Adjudication, recorded as a gap in the model 'droht ihm mit Krieg, falls der Gefangene nicht freigelassen werde' is a threat; no act type of rv:ActTypes means a threat, and forcing it into rv:Communication would change its meaning. A condition needs a factoid to attach to. The extractor already reports the gap.
  • Adjudication, corrected conservatively The passage 'klagt ... über die Gefangennahme seines Gesandten' makes the envoy the object of the complaint. rv:Envoy (who is sent or delivers a message) describes a mission this factoid does not record. No more specific role fits, so rv:Participant is the safe fix.
  • Adjudication, recorded as a gap in the model The guidelines say the ruler uses the shared ruler IRI, and the actor label and nameInSource keep the question mark 'Friedrich?'. rf:qualifier qualifies the type or time of a factoid, not an identification, so the contract cannot mark an uncertain identification. The batch report already lists the identification as unresolved.
  • Correction Changed rf:role of <urn:regesta:22732:ref-gesandter> from rv:Envoy to rv:Participant; actor and nameInSource "seines Gesandten" unchanged.
  • Correction Set prov:wasAttributedTo of <urn:regesta:22732:complaint> to the corrector.
  • Gap in the model, extraction Threat of war with condition 'falls der Gefangene nicht freigelassen werde' not recorded: no act type for a threat; military content otherwise out of scope
  • Gap in the model, extraction The capture of the envoy itself (hostage/prisoner taking) not recorded as out of scope; only the complaint about it

  • Gap in the model, review No way to mark an actor identification that the regest itself flags as uncertain.
