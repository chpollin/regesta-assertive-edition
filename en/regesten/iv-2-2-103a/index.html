RI IV,2,2 n. 103a(1152 Juni/Juli)Regensburg

TEI Turtle JSON

Vor König Friedrich (coram rege Friderico) wird der Streit zwischen Eufemia, der Witwe Heinrichs von Hegilen, und dem Stift Berchtesgaden unter Propst Heinrich um Saline und Salzpfanne zu Reichenhall nach dem Zeugnis der Edelfreien Otto von Moos, Erbo von Biburg und Herbord von Hebingen durch Spruch des erwählten Richters Markgraf Engelbert von Kraiburg (Istrien) zugunsten des Stiftes entschieden. Nennung lokaler Zeugen, darunter Markgraf Engelbert von Kraiburg, Kuno von Mödling (Megelingen), Babo von Zulling (Zollingen), Pilgrim von Wolmotsa, Otto von Moos, Erbo von Biburg und Wernhard von Hornbach.

Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Gap in the model, extraction The regest does not say which party brought the dispute, so both parties carry rv:Participant; the vocabulary has no neutral role for a party to a dispute.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No role for the head under whom a community appears as party (Stift Berchtesgaden unter Propst Heinrich); recorded as a separate rv:OfficeHolding factoid and Heinrich kept out of the dispute roles.
  • Gap in the model, extraction The ruler's presence at the judgment (Vor König Friedrich, coram rege Friderico) has no specific role and is recorded as rv:Participant.
  • Gap in the model, extraction The basis of the judgment (nach dem Zeugnis der Edelfreien ...) is carried only by rv:Witness references, not by rf:ground.
  • Gap in the model, extraction Saline und Salzpfanne zu Reichenhall is a regest-local crm:E18_Physical_Thing; the register entry RI004-002-R000410 (Salzpfanne) is not linked because the model has no link from a thing to a register entry.
