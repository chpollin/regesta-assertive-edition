Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The investiture stands in the subjunctive of reported speech ('investiert worden sei'): the regest reports what the pope's letter asserts, not a fact the regest itself narrates. The factoid records it as a plain Appointment without any trace of this framing. The qualifiers keep 'auch schon' but drop the 'sei' that marks the report.
  • Finding Two statements are merged: Wichmann obtained the Magdeburg see through royal favour, and the king invested him. 'durch königliche Gunst' is stored as a qualifier, although it describes the means of acquiring the see rather than qualifying the investiture.
  • Finding The threat carries no references; the threatening pope and the threatened chapter are only reachable through rf:partOf.
  • Adjudication, corrected conservatively The regest gives the investiture in the subjunctive of the papal letter: 'vom König auch schon investiert worden sei', while 'zu verschaffen wußte' is indicative. The contract has no claim-attribution mechanism (model gap), but rf:qualifier can keep the exact mood-bearing wording so the factoid does not read as narrated fact.
  • Adjudication, kept 'durch königliche Gunst' states the means, not a reason the regest gives, so rf:ground would misclassify it; rf:qualifier keeps the exact wording. One Appointment factoid is defensible for 'den Magdeburger Stuhl zu verschaffen wußte und vom König auch schon investiert worden sei'.
  • Adjudication, not required The provision is linked by rf:partOf to the command, which carries the pope as Grantor and the chapter as Addressee; the guidelines do not require repeating references on a provision.
  • Correction Added rf:qualifier "investiert worden sei" to <urn:regesta:22753:investitur>, keeping "durch königliche Gunst" and "auch schon"; producer set to correction-g7 (adjudication: correct_conservatively).
  • Gap in the model, extraction The royal investiture of Wichmann is reported in the pope's indirect speech ('worden sei'); the model has no way to mark a factoid as a party's assertion within the source, so it stands as an ordinary Appointment factoid.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No act type for a command or threat of sanction; both are recorded as rv:Enactment.
  • Gap in the model, extraction The Magdeburg see and 'Amt und Pfründe' are modelled as E30 rights of type rv:ChurchRights; whether an episcopal see is a right is an expert question.
  • Gap in the model, review No way to mark a factoid as the reported assertion of an actor within the regest (reported speech in a letter), as distinct from what the regest narrates.
  • Open identity 'vom König' is identified with the packet's shared ruler (Friedrich Barbarossa); the regest does not name him in this passage.
