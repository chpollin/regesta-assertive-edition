RI IV,2,1 n. 811152 (Ende April-Anfang Mai)Paderborn (Padeburne)

TEI Turtle JSON

Friedrich bestätigt auf Veranlassung des Klosters S. Remi zu Reims (ęcclesia sancti Remigii) und nach dem schriftlichen Bericht (scripti attestatione) seiner Gesandten, des Dekans R(icher) von (St. Adalbert zu) Aachen, des Schultheißen A. und seines Marschalls Macelinus, die Rechte des Reiches an dem diesem Kloster gehörenden Hof Meerssen (Marnensi), um die Streitigkeiten mit dem Vogt, Goswin von Heinsberg, beizulegen; im einzelnen werden die Abgaben und deren Aufteilung auf den König, den Grafen und den Hof bei den drei jährlichen Gerichtstagen sowie die Vogtei über diesen Hof geregelt. Z.: Erzbischof Arnold von Köln, Bischof Bernhard von Paderborn, der Kanzler Arnold, der Notar Heinrich, Herzog Welf, Markgraf Albrecht (der Bär), Graf Ulrich von Lenzburg, Markward (wohl von Grumbach). - Data Padeburne per manus Arnoldi canc. ; außerhalb der Kanzlei verfaßt und geschrieben; SP. 1. Ad precidendas occasiones .

Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding rf:refersToAct links the confirmation to the envoys' report. The contract defines refersToAct for a later act such as a confirmation referring to an earlier act; read that way, the candidate implies that Friedrich confirms the report. The regest says he confirms the rights on the basis of the report (nach dem schriftlichen Bericht), which is a basis, not the object of the confirmation.
  • Finding The envoys are Friedrich's (seiner Gesandten) and report to him; Friedrich is not referenced in the report factoid. The regest implies but does not state an addressee.
  • Finding The provisions are passive (werden ... geregelt) and carry no grantor. This is faithful to the wording; Friedrich as grantor follows only from the main act via rf:partOf.
  • Finding "den König" in the revenue division is modelled as a functional actor rather than the shared ruler. This is defensible, since the share belongs to the king as office; it avoids asserting identity with Friedrich. Likewise "den Grafen" is not identified with Goswin, which the passage does not support.
  • Adjudication, kept rf:refersToAct is defined as a later act referring to an earlier act of the same regest; it does not say 'confirms'. The object of the confirmation is given by rf:concerns (the rights of the Empire at Meerssen), and 'nach dem schriftlichen Bericht' does refer to that report, so the link is defensible. A dedicated basis property is a model gap.
  • Adjudication, not required 'seiner Gesandten' implies but does not state to whom the report went; the contract does not require an inferred addressee.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No actor for 'das Reich' as holder of 'die Rechte des Reiches'; no RightHolding factoid was built and the right is typed only with the top concept rv:LordshipRights.
  • Gap in the model, extraction The parties of 'die Streitigkeiten mit dem Vogt' other than Goswin are not stated; only Goswin is referenced as Participant. Goswin's Vogt office is recorded as OfficeHolding with qualifier 'Vogt' without its object.
  • Gap in the model, extraction rf:refersToAct links the confirmation to the envoys' written report on which it is based; the model has no distinct 'based on' relation.
  • Gap in the model, review No property for the evidentiary basis of an act (nach dem schriftlichen Bericht) distinct from refersToAct.
  • Gap in the model, review No role for the beneficiary share of a thing (den Hof) as opposed to an actor.
  • Open identity 'den König' and 'den Grafen' in the provision on Abgaben are modelled as FunctionalActors, not as the ruler and not as Goswin; whether 'den Grafen' means the Vogt Goswin is unresolved.
