Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The holding 'aus der Hand des Königs' is kept as a qualifier only. It states that the Vogtei derives from the king, which an expert might read as a feudal or delegated relation with the king as lord. The passage does not say 'zu Lehen', so not modelling a FeudalRelationship is defensible; the derivation from the king is recorded only as a literal.
  • Finding The 'älteren Privilegien seiner Vorgänger' are earlier acts that the order relies on. They appear only inside rf:ground. Since the passage names neither the predecessors nor the content of the privileges, leaving them unmodelled is acceptable.
  • Finding Wasseiges and Evrehailles have register candidates in this packet (RI004-002-W000192, RI004-002-E000618) but receive regest-local IRIs. The guidelines regulate actor identity only, so this is not a violation, but it loses a linkage the packet offers.
  • Adjudication, recorded as a gap in the model The passage 'aus der Hand des Königs innehat' does not say 'zu Lehen', so rv:FeudalRelationship with rv:Lord would overread it, and the role scheme has no concept for the source of a held right. The qualifier keeps the exact wording; no correction.
  • Adjudication, not required The guidelines (Actors and identity) regulate register IRIs for actors only; places are nodes 'crm:E53_Place, each with an rdfs:label' under urn:regesta:UID:SLUG. Whether places should take register IRIs is a contract question for the next version, not a defect of this file.
  • Gap in the model, extraction Holding a right 'aus der Hand des Königs' has no lord or source-of-tenure role short of rv:FeudalRelationship, which the wording does not establish; kept as rf:qualifier
  • Gap in the model, extraction The older privileges of the predecessors are cited only as ground; their content is not stated, so no earlier act factoid was created
  • Gap in the model, review No role or property for the origin of a held right (a right held 'aus der Hand des Königs') short of a full feudal relationship.
  • Open identity 'des Königs' in 'aus der Hand des Königs' is kept as qualifier only; whether it names Friedrich himself or the kingship generally is not resolved
  • Open identity 'seiner Vorgänger' (predecessors whose older privileges were inspected) not modelled as actors
