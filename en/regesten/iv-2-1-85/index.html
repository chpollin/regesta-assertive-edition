Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The factoid makes Friedrich the kin of Duke Heinrich. In the passage 'seines Verwandten' follows directly on 'des dortigen Propstes Benno', so the possessive can grammatically point to Benno as well as to the sentence subject Friedrich. Regest convention favours Friedrich, but the second selector 'Friedrich bestätigt' only shows that Friedrich is the subject, not that the possessive refers to him; the register label 'Cousin Friedrich Barbarossas' may not be used as evidence. The reading is plausible but is an interpretation that the file does not mark.
  • Finding 'Obsorge' of the bishop over the Stift is typed rv:LordshipRights. This is a defensible top-level choice; a more specific type does not exist in the scheme.
  • Finding The witness list states several kinship relations (Lutolf and his sons, Hoger as brother of Lutolf, Rudolf de Capella and his son Ulrich). Omitted per the witness-list exclusion; noted as a boundary case because these are stated relations, not formulae.
  • Adjudication, kept In 'auf Bitten Bischof Bernhards von Hildesheim, dessen Obsorge das Stift untersteht, des dortigen Propstes Benno und seines Verwandten' the regest uses 'dessen' for a non-subject antecedent and 'seines' for the sentence subject Friedrich; the candidate's reading follows this grammar and does not rest on the register label. The model cannot mark uncertainty inside a factoid; the residual doubt belongs to expert review, not to correction.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No right type for the bishop's 'Obsorge' over the Stift; rv:LordshipRights (top concept) used as the nearest group.
  • Gap in the model, extraction Witness list states kinship (Graf Lutolf von Wöltingerode and his sons Lutolf and Burchard, Hoger his brother, Rudolf de Capella and his son Ulrich); not extracted because the guidelines exclude formulaic witness lists.
  • Gap in the model, review An ambiguous antecedent cannot be recorded inside a factoid; the model has no way to mark an identification as uncertain other than the batch report.
  • Gap in the model, review Kinship inside witness lists is excluded by rule, not by content.
  • Open identity 'seines Verwandten, des Herzogs Heinrich von Sachsen und Bayern': 'seines' read as referring to the subject Friedrich; grammatically it could also refer to Propst Benno.
