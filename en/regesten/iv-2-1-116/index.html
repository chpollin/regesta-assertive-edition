Friedrich nimmt die genannten Grafen von Castello (Mainfredum, Caualkasellam et fratres et Ardicionem et Crollamontem filios Wilielmi et Guidonis comites de Castello), wie seine Vorgänger Karl, Otto und Heinrich in seinen Schutz, bestätigt ihnen ihre Besitzungen und Rechte vor allem am Westufer des Lago Maggiore und im Tale Ossola (Oxola) und nimmt auch die Eigengüter der Gattin Manfreds von Castello, Berta, in seinen Schutz. C; SI. D. Regalis munificentię dignitatis .

Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The name list states kinship: Cavalcasella and his brothers (fratres), and Ardicio and Crollamons as sons of Wilhelm and Guido. Wilhelm (RI004-002-W000559) and Guido (RI004-002-G001184) are register candidates of this packet. The distributive reading of filios Wilielmi et Guidonis is ambiguous, so a Parenthood with one parent per child would overreach, but the brother relation and the filiation could be recorded as rv:Kinship with the exact word in rf:qualifier, or the ambiguity reported.
  • Finding Friedrich confirms Besitzungen und Rechte; only the Besitzungen appear as a rf:concerns node. The rights are dropped.
  • Finding Berta's possession of the Eigengüter is supported by the genitive, but it duplicates the object of the protection factoid. Acceptable.
  • Adjudication, recorded as a gap in the model In 'Caualkasellam et fratres et Ardicionem et Crollamontem filios Wilielmi et Guidonis' it is open whose brothers 'fratres' are, which children 'filios' governs and how they pair with the two fathers. The guidelines require rv:Parenthood with one parent and one child, and forbid substituting rv:Kinship for a parent relation. Any factoid would therefore over- or under-claim. The ambiguity is already in the batch report.
  • Adjudication, recorded as a gap in the model 'bestätigt ihnen ihre Besitzungen und Rechte' names unspecified rights. RightShape requires a crm:P2_has_type from rv:RightTypes, and no generic right type exists, while picking a top concept would be an unsupported judgement. The gap is reported.
  • Gap in the model, extraction filios Wilielmi et Guidonis states parenthood, but the pairing of children to the fathers Wilhelm and Guido and the extent of the list it governs are ambiguous; no Parenthood or Kinship factoid was written.
  • Gap in the model, extraction The confirmed 'Rechte' have no specific right type in rv:RightTypes and no generic type exists; they are not modelled as an E30_Right.
  • Gap in the model, extraction Manfred in the marriage passage and Mainfredum among the recipients are both identified with register candidate M000332 by name and title, each from this packet.
  • Gap in the model, review No generic right type for unspecified 'Rechte' in rv:RightTypes.
