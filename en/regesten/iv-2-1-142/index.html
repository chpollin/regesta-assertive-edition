Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The guideline turns a stated parent relation into a Parenthood factoid; "seine Eltern" of Friedrich is extracted only as a Beneficiary group, so the parent relation is missing.
  • Finding The realm (regno) is modelled as an E74_Group actor and possessor; this is an interpretive move, and "attinet" expresses belonging rather than possession in the strict sense.
  • Finding Bischof Gunther appears only through the reason for the donation; rv:Participant is acceptable, as the passage does not make him recipient.
  • Adjudication, corrected The guidelines make a stated parent relation a rv:Parenthood state factoid; 'Friedrich schenkt als Seelgerät für seine Eltern' states it as plainly as 22763, where it was extracted. The local group <urn:regesta:actor:22769:local:eltern> already exists.
  • Adjudication, kept 'castrum Berwartstein, quod regno ac nobis attinet' names realm and ruler as those the castle belongs to; rv:Possession with rf:qualifier "attinet" keeps the wording. A category for the realm as property holder is a gap for the next model version.
  • Correction Added <urn:regesta:22769:parenthood> (rv:Parenthood; local group seine Eltern rv:Parent, Friedrich rv:Child; selector "Friedrich schenkt als Seelgerät für seine Eltern"); producer correction-g7. Beneficiary reference on the donation kept.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No role for the person whose merits ground an act (Bischof Gunther, 'wegen der treuen Dienste'); recorded as rv:Participant plus rf:ground.
  • Gap in the model, extraction Seelgerät beneficiaries recorded with rv:Beneficiary; the model has no memorial or soul-gift type distinct from rv:Donation.
  • Gap in the model, review No actor category for the realm (regnum) as a holder of property.
  • Open identity Recipient 'bischöflichen Kirche von Speyer' identified with candidate RI004-002-S000970 (typed ort, label 'Bischöfliche Kirche St. Maria, Bistum') as community; the candidate is a place entry.
