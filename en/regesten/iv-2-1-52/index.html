Commentary Die Urkunde ist in zwei Exemplaren erhalten; vgl. zum Verdacht der Fälschung und zu der Bedeutung für die Frühgeschichte von Eußerthal zuletzt Doll, Eußerthal, Mitt. HV Pfalz 68 (1970) 203 ff. - Die Datierung ergibt sich aus der Nennung Bischof Gebhards von Würzburg in der Zeugenreihe, der erst nach dem 16. September 1150 zum Bischof gewählt wurde, vgl. Bernhardi, Konrad III. 858 mit Anm. 49 und Wendehorst, Bistum Würzburg (Germ. sacra N. F. 1, 1962) 154 f. - Friedrich bestätigte diese Übergabe an Eußerthal im Jahre 1186, wobei Teile der Bischofsurkunde in dieses Diplom wörtlich übernommen wurden, Stumpf 4469 von 1186 November 11, vgl. Doll, a. a. O., 205 ff.

Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The handover of an endowment (Ausstattung) to a monastery is a gratuitous transfer to a church, which the narrower rv:Donation covers; rv:Grant is correct but less specific.
  • Finding King Konrad is the ruler before whom the handover is performed. rv:Participant is the only available fit; the vocabulary has no role for the authority before whom an act takes place.
  • Finding Rapoto's later office is kept only in the name literal. An rv:OfficeHolding factoid with rf:qualifier "späteren" would be expressible, but the guidelines restrict office holding to where the statement needs it, so leaving it out is defensible.
  • Finding The endowment is typed crm:E18_Physical_Thing. The regest gives no content of the Ausstattung, which may comprise rights as well as land; the type is a reading, though the contract offers nothing better.
  • Adjudication, kept 'Übergabe der Ausstattung von Eußerthal an das dortige Kloster' does not state that the transfer was gratuitous, which rv:Donation presupposes. The broader rv:Grant with qualifier 'Übergabe' asserts no more than the passage.
  • Adjudication, not required 'Rapoto, den späteren Bischof von Speyer' names an office held later, not at the act; the guidelines restrict office holding to where the statement needs it, and the wording is kept in nameInSource and label.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No role for the ruler before whom an act is performed (vor König Konrad); recorded as rv:Participant.
  • Gap in the model, extraction Charter witness (Zeuge) has no in-scope role; Friedrich is not referenced.
  • Gap in the model, extraction Übergabe der Ausstattung recorded as rv:Grant, not rv:Donation, because the regest does not state that it was gratuitous.
