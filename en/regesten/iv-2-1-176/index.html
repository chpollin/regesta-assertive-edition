Friedrich bestätigt auf Bitten des 'Bruders' Johannes (des Stiftsverwalters) dem Stift Fredelsloh (cenobium Fridessele) in Erneuerung der Verfügungen Konrads (III.) als Seelgerät die dem Reich gehörige Kapelle Grone, den Königsschutz, die ausschließliche Unterstellung unter die königliche Vogtei (ne advocatum aliquem preter nos nostrosque successores reges seu imperatores predictus locus habeat) und fügt selber die Schenkung zweier Hufen mit dem Ertrag von 20 Solidi, sowie einer Mühle mit 4 Solidi Ertrag in Roßdorf (R잒sthorp) hinzu. Z.: Bischof Konrad von Augsburg, Propst Gottschalk (von Heiligenstadt), Notar Heinrich, die Kapelläne Hartwich (Domkantor und Propst von St. Johann zu Mainz), Reinbert, Jordan und Heribert, Herzog Heinrich (von Sachsen), die Grafen Rudolf von Bregenz und Otto von Ravensberg, Markward von Grumbach, Reinboto von Röckingen, die Ministerialen Gerwich von Kiffhausen (Cuphese), Kapellar Friedrich, Kapellar Widekind und Kapellar Azzo. - Arnoldus canc. ; in Wiederholung des DK. III. 149 (VU.), bis auf das von A II. D stammende Monogramm, von einem Empfängerschreiber mundiert, der auch DF. I. 57 (Reg. 177) schrieb; SI. 1. Si antecessorum nostrorum .

Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding Both amounts sit as literals on one donation factoid that concerns two hides, a mill and Roßdorf, so the reading that 20 Solidi belong to the hides and 4 Solidi to the mill is lost. The contract could keep it by two donation factoids, one per item, each with its own amount.
  • Finding Konrad III's earlier act carries only the grantor. The regest says Friedrich renews these dispositions in favour of Fredelsloh, so the stift as recipient and the chapel, protection and advocacy as concerned items rest on the same clause. Typing it rv:Enactment rather than rv:Grant is cautious and defensible.
  • Finding The regest marks the donation as Friedrich's own addition beyond the renewal ('fügt selber ... hinzu'). rf:partOf the confirmation is admissible as a secondary act, but it blurs that this is a new grant, not part of what is confirmed.
  • Finding 'als Seelgerät' states the pious purpose of the act rather than a type or time qualification; rf:ground would fit it better. The exclusivity of royal advocacy is kept only inside the right label and evidence, not as a literal.
  • Adjudication, corrected conservatively The finding holds: 'zweier Hufen mit dem Ertrag von 20 Solidi, sowie einer Mühle mit 4 Solidi Ertrag' binds each yield to its item. Splitting one stated act into two factoids would change the unit of the act to work around the model (rf:amount attaches to the factoid). The safe fix keeps one donation and binds the yields in the labels of the concerned things, which the guidelines require to be in the source's words.
  • Adjudication, kept rf:partOf is defined for 'a provision or secondary act belonging to a main act of the same regest'; the donation is added in the same charter ('fügt selber ... hinzu'), and rf:qualifier "selber" keeps the addition visible.
  • Adjudication, kept 'als Seelgerät' qualifies the kind of transfer rather than giving a reason, and its exact wording is kept; the contract has no purpose property. 'preter nos ...' is not an exception to the confirmation but the content of the exclusive royal advocacy, kept in the right's label and the evidence.
  • Adjudication, not required 'in Erneuerung der Verfügungen Konrads (III.)' does not state what Konrad disposed or for whom; carrying the present items back to the earlier act would infer its content.
  • Correction rdfs:label of <urn:regesta:22803:hufen> changed to "zweier Hufen mit dem Ertrag von 20 Solidi".
  • Correction rdfs:label of <urn:regesta:22803:muehle> changed to "einer Mühle mit 4 Solidi Ertrag".
  • Correction Factoid urn:regesta:22803:donation left unchanged, including both rf:amount literals and its producer, because only the labels of the concerned things changed and the instruction keeps the factoid unchanged.
  • Gap in the model, extraction The content of Konrad's earlier 'Verfügungen' is only implied by 'in Erneuerung'; the earlier act is typed rv:Enactment without rf:concerns, since the regest does not state which items Konrad granted.
  • Gap in the model, extraction rf:amount cannot be bound to an individual thing: '20 Solidi' belongs to the two Hufen, '4 Solidi' to the mill, both sit on one donation factoid. The amounts are yields (Ertrag), not prices, and the model has no property for revenue yield.
  • Gap in the model, extraction 'als Seelgerät' (pious purpose) has no dedicated property; kept as rf:qualifier on the confirmation.
  • Gap in the model, review rf:amount attaches to the factoid, not to a concerned thing, so several amounts in one act cannot be bound to their items.
  • Gap in the model, review No property for the purpose of an act (Seelgerät, pro remedio animae) distinct from rf:ground.
