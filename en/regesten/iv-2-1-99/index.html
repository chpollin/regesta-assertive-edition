Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The confirmation covers two objects, the Besitzstand and the right to accept donations of allodial goods. Only the Besitzstand is a concerned node. The contract could carry the right as a crm:E30_Right with an rdfs:label in the source wording and the nearest draft right type (for example a top concept), reporting the poor fit as a model gap, instead of leaving it only in the evidence.
  • Finding 'nach dem Beispiel seiner Vorgänger' points to earlier confirmations by predecessors. The extractor keeps it as a qualifier only, whereas the analogous 'schon von seinen Vorgängern' in 22729 became an earlier act. Both readings are defensible; the treatment should be uniform across the run.
  • Adjudication, recorded as a gap in the model The passage confirms 'das Recht, Schenkungen von Allodialgütern anzunehmen', but a crm:E30_Right must carry a concept of rv:RightTypes and none fits a capacity to acquire donations; forcing the nearest concept would be a historical judgement the correction prompt excludes ('Where the vocabulary does not suffice, leave the detail out and record the gap').
  • Adjudication, kept The passages differ: 22726 states only a precedent ('nach dem Beispiel seiner Vorgänger'), whereas 22729 reports an earlier act ('die schon von seinen Vorgängern ... ausgezeichnet worden war'). Different modelling follows the wording.
  • Gap in the model, extraction The confirmed right to accept donations of allodial goods (das Recht, Schenkungen von Allodialgütern anzunehmen) fits no concept of rv:RightTypes; left untyped, only in the evidence.
  • Gap in the model, extraction "nach dem Beispiel seiner Vorgänger" kept as rf:qualifier; no earlier act created, referent of "seiner" not resolved.
  • Gap in the model, review No right type for a capacity to acquire or accept donations.
  • Gap in the model, review Kinship stated inside witness lists ('seine Söhne Otto und Friedrich', 'sein Bruder') is excluded by the source boundary and therefore lost.
