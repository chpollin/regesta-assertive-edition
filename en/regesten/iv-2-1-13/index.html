Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding A consent to a foundation is typed rv:Confirmation. The vocabulary defines a confirmation as confirming possession, rights or an earlier act; the regest reports consent to an Errichtung, not the confirmation of an existing state or prior act. Querying confirmations would wrongly include this record. The extractor marked the choice as nearest type, and the qualifier "Zustimmung" preserves the wording, so the defect is partly mitigated.
  • Finding Konstantin and Giselbert are the founders of the Stift to which consent is given; rv:Beneficiary is defensible as the party in whose favour the consent is given, but loses that they are the actors of the consented act.
  • Finding The label keeps the genitive inflection "Frauenstiftes Lochgarten". It is the source wording, as required, but reads awkwardly as a label.
  • Adjudication, corrected conservatively The passage reads 'seine Zustimmung zur Errichtung des Frauenstiftes Lochgarten durch die Untertanen von Lorch, Konstantin und Giselbert'. rv:Confirmation is defined as confirming 'Besitz, Rechte oder einen früheren Akt', and the guidelines model a confirmation as two act factoids joined by rf:refersToAct; the regest reports no earlier act or existing right that is confirmed, only consent to an act of others. The vocabulary has no consent type and no generic act type, so the current typing turns a model limit into a different historical meaning, which correct.md forbids, and the qualifier 'Zustimmung' does not undo the false membership in queries for confirmations. Reclassifying under another type would be a new reading. The only safe fix is removal with a recorded gap. The Vogtei holding and the parenthood factoid of the same passage are supported and stay.
  • Correction Removed the act factoid <urn:regesta:22640:consent> (typed rv:Confirmation) with all its triples and its two preceding comment lines, following adjudication-g1 decision correct_conservatively.
  • Correction Removed the reference nodes <urn:regesta:22640:consent-ref-friedrich>, <urn:regesta:22640:consent-ref-father>, <urn:regesta:22640:consent-ref-konstantin> and <urn:regesta:22640:consent-ref-giselbert>.
  • Correction Removed the actor nodes <urn:regesta:actor:RI004-002-K000569>, <urn:regesta:actor:RI004-002-G000630> and <urn:regesta:actor:RI004-002-L000545:community>, which no remaining triple references.
  • Correction Kept <urn:regesta:22640:parenthood>, <urn:regesta:22640:vogtei-holding>, their references, <urn:regesta:22640:right-vogtei-lorch> and <urn:regesta:actor:RI004-002-L000660:community> byte-for-byte unchanged with their original producer; no factoid was added or changed, so no factoid carries the correction producer.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No act type for consent (Zustimmung): the consent of Friedrich and his father is typed rv:Confirmation with rf:qualifier "Zustimmung" as nearest fit; a dedicated consent type is needed.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No act type for the foundation of a religious house (Errichtung des Frauenstiftes Lochgarten): the foundation by Konstantin and Giselbert is not recorded as an act of its own, only as the object of the consent, with both as rv:Beneficiary references; a founder role is missing.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No state type for personal dependency or subjection (Untertanen von Lorch): the status of Konstantin and Giselbert as subjects of Lorch is not recorded.
  • Gap in the model, extraction The capacity in which the consent is given (als Inhaber der Vogtei) is recorded as a separate RightHolding factoid; the contract has no property linking an act to the capacity that grounds it.
  • Gap in the model, review No act type for consent (Zustimmung) to another party's act.
  • Gap in the model, review No act type for the foundation (Errichtung) of a church institution, and no founder role.
  • Gap in the model, review No state type for the dependency of Konstantin and Giselbert as Untertanen of Kloster Lorch.
