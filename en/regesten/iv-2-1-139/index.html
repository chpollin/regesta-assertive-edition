RI IV,2,1 n. 1391152 Oktober 17Würzburg

TEI Turtle JSON

Friedrich investiert auf Bitten Bischof Uguccios von Vercelli und seines Kanzlers Arnold den Walo di Casalvolone (Casale Walonis) mit den Reichslehen, nämlich der Banngewalt zu Casalvolone, Rosasco, Serga, Pezzana und Buronzo, jeweils mit dem Weiderecht und den übrigen Regalien (districtum Casalis Walonis cum pascuis et aliis ad regalia pertinentibus, districtum in Rosasco cum pascuis et aliis regalibus, districtum in Serga et in Petiana et in Burontio cum pascuis et aliis ad regalia pertinentibus.), erlaubt ihm die Rückgewinnung von durch seine Vorgänger entfremdeten Lehen mit Ausnahme derjenigen Alberts und Heinrichs sowie ihrer Brüder, falls diese nichts von den Regalien veräußert haben, verleiht ihm das Befestigungsrecht und bestätigt ihm alle Privilegien seiner Vorgänger. Z.: Erzbischof Hartwig (Hartuicus) von Bremen, die Bischöfe Uguccio von Vercelli, Heinrich von Regensburg, Eberhard von Bamberg, die Herzoge Heinrich von Sachsen, Welf, Matthäus von Lothringen, Markgraf Albrecht (der Bär) von Sachsen, Graf Wido von Biandrate, Cavalcasella von Castel d'Agogno (Castello). - Arnoldus canc. vice Henrici Mogontini archiep. et archicanc. ; verfaßt und nach der Nachzeichnung in B auch mundiert von A II. D; nach Beschreibung in B bereits damals beschädigtes SI. 1. Notum sit omnibus .

Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding "seiner Vorgänger" is ambiguous between Friedrich's predecessors as issuers and Walo's predecessors as holders of the privileges. Giving the functional actor the role Grantor asserts issuance, which the passage does not decide, and the label does not say whose predecessors are meant.
  • Finding One Kinship factoid with Albert, Heinrich and their brothers as three Kin references reads as one relation among all three and thus suggests that Albert and Heinrich are related to each other, which the passage leaves open; the comment in the file acknowledges this but the data do not.
  • Adjudication, corrected conservatively In 'bestätigt ihm alle Privilegien seiner Vorgänger' the possessive may refer to Friedrich (issuing predecessors) or to Walo (predecessors holding the privileges); in the same sentence 'durch seine Vorgänger entfremdeten Lehen' is read as Walo's. The passage does not decide, so rv:Participant is the safe role.
  • Adjudication, corrected 'Alberts und Heinrichs sowie ihrer Brüder' states that each has brothers but not that Albert and Heinrich are related; a three-party Kinship factoid asserts one relation among all three, against the guideline not to build relations the text does not state.
  • Correction In <urn:regesta:22766:earlier-privileges> changed the role of <urn:regesta:actor:22766:local:vorgaenger-privilegien> from rv:Grantor to rv:Participant; actor, label and nameInSource "seiner Vorgänger" kept; producer correction-g7.
  • Correction Removed <urn:regesta:22766:kinship-brueder>; added <urn:regesta:22766:kinship-brueder-albert> (Alberts and ihrer Brüder, rv:Kin) and <urn:regesta:22766:kinship-brueder-heinrich> (Heinrichs and ihrer Brüder, rv:Kin), each rv:Kinship with qualifier "Brüder"; producer correction-g7.
  • Gap in the model, extraction 'Alberts und Heinrichs sowie ihrer Brüder' does not say whether Albert and Heinrich are brothers of each other; the kinship factoid links both with an unnamed group 'ihrer Brüder' and asserts no chain.
  • Gap in the model, extraction The condition 'falls diese nichts von den Regalien veräußert haben' attaches to the exception, not to the permission; the model cannot attach a condition to an exception.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No right type for fortification (Befestigungsrecht) or for regalia in general; mapped to rv:LordshipRights.
  • Gap in the model, review No right type for the right of fortification or for Banngewalt as distinct from jurisdiction; they are approximated with LordshipRights and Jurisdiction.
  • Open identity Albert and Heinrich identified with the register entries RI004-002-A000276 and RI004-002-H000721, which carry no title ([k.A.]); match by name only.
  • Open identity 'seiner Vorgänger' in 'alle Privilegien seiner Vorgänger' is ambiguous between Friedrich's and Walo's predecessors; kept as a separate functional actor from Walo's predecessors in the recovery clause.
