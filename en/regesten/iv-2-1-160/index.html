TEI Turtle JSON

Friedrich spricht dem Ovdelhardus von Vivers auf Grund eines Fürstenspruches die Vogtei über den Hof des Klosters Peterlingen zu Kerzers (Chiètres; Kerters) ab, befreit es von allen vogteirechtlichen Ansprüchen (aliquis aliquod ius advocatię sibi usurpare presumat), bestätigt dem Kloster das von seinen Vorgängern verliehene Vorrecht, daß der Abt von Cluny und seine Nachfolger den von den Mönchen erwählten Vogt einzusetzen haben, und verbrieft den zum Teil namentlich angeführten Besitzstand. Z.: die Erzbischöfe Arnold von Köln, Humbert von Besançon, die Bischöfe Amadeus von Lausanne, Ortlieb von Basel, Hermann von Konstanz, Herzog Heinrich von Sachsen, Markgraf Hermann von Sachsen, die Grafen Amadeus von Genf, Wilhelm von Mâcon (Matisconensis). - Arnoldus canc. ; verfaßt und geschrieben von A II. D; SI. 1. Cum ęcclesiis dei .

Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding 'befreit es' takes a neuter antecedent and refers to the monastery of Peterlingen, not to the court (der Hof, masculine) at Kerzers. The candidate restricts the exemption from all advocacy claims to the court and names no beneficiary, so it states a narrower act than the passage does.
  • Finding The ruler's denial rests on a judgment of the princes. Keeping 'auf Grund eines Fürstenspruches' only as rf:ground collapses the princes' sentence into the ruler's act and makes Friedrich the sole rv:Judge.
  • Finding The packet has a register entry for the abbot of Cluny (C000775, 'Abt'). The candidate uses a local functional actor instead, which suits 'und seine Nachfolger'; in 22783 the analogous abbot of Conques got the register IRI. The run treats the two cases differently.
  • Finding The confirmation of the property names no recipient; the monastery is implied by the coordinated clause.
  • Adjudication, kept In 'die Vogtei über den Hof des Klosters Peterlingen zu Kerzers ... ab, befreit es von allen vogteirechtlichen Ansprüchen' the neuter pronoun can refer to 'Klosters Peterlingen' or to the place name 'Kerzers', which is neuter in German. The reviewer's argument from gender overlooks the place name, and the immediate context (the advocacy over the court is denied, then 'es' is freed from advocacy claims) supports the candidate's reading. The reading stays open for expert review.
  • Adjudication, kept 'auf Grund eines Fürstenspruches' is a reason the regest gives for Friedrich's act, which is exactly what rf:ground records in exact wording. The passage names no princes; creating a judging actor from the compound 'Fürstenspruch' would add an actor the text does not name. Friedrich pronounces the denial ('spricht ... ab'), so rv:Judge stands.
  • Adjudication, not required The guideline adds an individual holder only 'where the passage names an individual holder too'. 'der Abt von Cluny und seine Nachfolger' names no individual, and C000775 is an office entry without a personal name, so it cannot be matched by name and title.
  • Adjudication, corrected In 'bestätigt dem Kloster das ... Vorrecht ... und verbrieft den zum Teil namentlich angeführten Besitzstand' the dative 'dem Kloster' governs both coordinated verbs. The recipient is expressible with the identification already used on urn:regesta:22787:confirmation-vorrecht, and the guidelines allow a second selector where a role rests on a different passage.
  • Correction On urn:regesta:22787:confirmation-besitzstand added a second selector 'bestätigt dem Kloster' and a reference to urn:regesta:actor:RI004-002-P000278:community as rv:Recipient, nameInSource 'Kloster'; producer set to correction-g8.
  • Gap in the model, extraction The 'Fürstenspruch' on which the judgment rests is kept as rf:ground; it is not recorded as a separate judgment act by the princes.
  • Gap in the model, extraction Right to install the advocate mapped to rv:Investiture; whether this belongs to investiture or advocacy rights is a judgement for expert review.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No role for electors; the monks who elect the Vogt carry rv:Participant.
  • Gap in the model, extraction 'Hof des Klosters Peterlingen' states a belonging of the Hof; no possession factoid recorded, to avoid deriving ownership from a dispute.
  • Gap in the model, review No role for the actor whose right is denied in a judgment other than rv:Respondent, which presupposes a suit the regest does not report.
  • Open identity 'der Abt von Cluny und seine Nachfolger' modelled as a local functional actor; register candidate RI004-002-C000775 (Cluny // Abt) is an office-level entry and not used as an individual.
  • Open identity Recipient of the prior grant by 'seinen Vorgängern' is not named in that clause; no recipient reference recorded.
