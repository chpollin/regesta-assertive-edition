RI IV,2,1 n. 197(1153 September)

TEI Turtle JSON

Friedrich dankt dem oströmischen Kaiser Manuel für dessen durch einen Boten übersandtes Schreiben, versichert ihn gemäß dem Rat seines Oheims, König Konrads III., der ihn zu seinem Nachfolger erklärt hat, seines Willens zu guten gegenseitigen Beziehungen, die er durch seine Heirat mit einer Verwandten Manuels befestigen will, kündigt ihm eine Eilbotschaft an (celeres et expeditos nuntios) und teilt ihm mit, daß er für den kommenden Sommer eine Expedition nach Sizilien und Apulien plane (et in superveniente estate, tempore videlicet, quo reges ad bella solent procedere, in fortitudine magna imperii nostri Alpes transire disposuimus). - Verfaßt von Wibald. Magnitudinis tuę litteras .

Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The unnamed relative of Manuel is referenced as rv:Participant of the assurance. The passage names her only as the intended partner of a planned marriage ("befestigen will"); she takes no part in the assurance, so the reference overstates her involvement.
  • Finding Manuel's earlier letter, sent through a messenger, is an act the regest reports (Communication from Manuel to Friedrich, messenger unnamed) and is not extracted.
  • Finding Friedrich's own letter (thanks, announcement of a fast embassy) is not recorded as a Communication; the assurance covers the substantive content. The announced embassy is only announced and must not become a Mission.
  • Finding Designation as successor is typed rv:Appointment, defined as installation in an office. A designation for future succession is weaker than installation; the qualifier keeps the exact wording, which limits the risk.
  • Adjudication, corrected In 'die er durch seine Heirat mit einer Verwandten Manuels befestigen will' she appears only in an intention of Friedrich; she takes no part in the assurance. The kinship factoid already carries her, and rf:qualifier "befestigen will" keeps the planned character.
  • Adjudication, not required 'für dessen durch einen Boten übersandtes Schreiben' states a letter without stated content or legal relation; the substantive content of Friedrich's letter is recorded as the assurance, the kinship and the designation. The run abstains from letters without in-scope content (22814), so neither is a central omission.
