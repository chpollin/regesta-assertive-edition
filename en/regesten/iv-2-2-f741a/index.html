Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The counts of Panizo are the party against whom action is requested and the alleged plunderers. rv:Participant hides that they are the opposing party; the vocabulary's rv:Respondent fits a party complained against better.
  • Finding The regest states that the goods belong to the church of Bologna, and the packet carries the candidate RI004-002-B001067 (Bologna, Bistum, Kirche). The thing node is labelled with the phrase, but no rv:Possession factoid names the church as possessor, so the economic relation is lost.
  • Adjudication, recorded as a gap in the model The factoid is a rv:Request ('bittet den Kaiser, gegen die Grafen von Panizo vorzugehen'), not a rv:Dispute, and rv:Respondent ('Beklagter') belongs to a lawsuit, which the regest does not report. No role covers the target of a requested action, so rv:Participant, the contract's role for involvement without a more specific role, stays; the extractor already reported the gap.
  • Adjudication, corrected The regest states 'die Güter der Bologneser Kirche', an economic relation in scope that rv:Possession and rv:Possessor express; the packet candidate RI004-002-B001067 'Bologna // Bistum, Diözese, Kirche' matches 'Bologneser Kirche' by name and kind. The same reading is used in this group for the monastery goods in 22861. The plundering itself stays only in rf:ground.
