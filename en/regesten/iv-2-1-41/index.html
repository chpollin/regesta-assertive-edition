Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding A treaty between two rulers is typed rv:Enactment with both parties as rv:Participant. Enactment is defined as a determination the charter makes; a bilateral agreement has no act type or contracting-party role in the vocabulary. The reading is acceptable as a stopgap and is a model gap, not an extraction error.
  • Finding The treaty's provision of joint action against King Roger is not a separate provision factoid and Roger (candidate R000806) carries no reference. The vocabulary has no role for the party a provision is directed against, and joint military action is close to the out-of-scope military boundary, so the omission is defensible. The provision is correctly not read as performed.
  • Adjudication, not required The provision 'der ein gemeinsames Vorgehen gegen König Roger von Sizilien vorsieht' is already inside the treaty's evidence and is not read as performed. A joint military action is near the out-of-scope military boundary, the vocabulary has no role for Roger, and rf:qualifier is for wording that qualifies type or time, not for the content of a provision.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No act type for a treaty or agreement between parties; the treaty between König Konrad III. and Kaiser Manuel is typed rv:Enactment and its parties carry rv:Participant.
  • Gap in the model, extraction The treaty's content 'ein gemeinsames Vorgehen gegen König Roger von Sizilien' has no slot (no property for the provided content and no role for the party an agreement is directed against); it stays in the evidence only and König Roger receives no reference.
  • Gap in the model, review No act type for a treaty or agreement and no role for contracting parties.
  • Gap in the model, review No role for the actor against whom an agreed action or provision is directed (Roger).
