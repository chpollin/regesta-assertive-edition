Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The exchange is typed rv:Grant, whose definition presupposes a one-directional transfer. With both parties as rv:Participant no direction is asserted, and the qualifier keeps Tauschgeschäft, so the reading stays within the passage. Friedrich's per manum role is rendered as rv:Mediator; rv:Representative would be equally defensible.
  • Finding The evidence selector starts inside the Latin parenthesis and omits the verb wirkt that carries Friedrich's participation; the support is complete only together with the office factoid's span.
  • Finding rf:qualifier "Herzog von Schwaben" names the office itself rather than qualifying type or time; the contract has no slot for the office. The temporal noch is correctly kept, and the later kingship (postea ... regis) is rightly not extracted as a state of this date.
  • Adjudication, kept The selector 'per manum Friderici, ... an einem Tauschgeschäft zwischen Graf Berthold von Plassenburg-Andechs und dem Zisterzienserkloster Langheim mit' is a contiguous substring that carries the exchange, both parties and Friedrich's per manum part; the guidelines ask for the shortest passage carrying type and roles.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No exchange act type: the Tauschgeschäft between Graf Berthold and Langheim is typed rv:Grant with qualifier 'Tauschgeschäft'; goods and direction are not stated, so both parties are rv:Participant.
