RI IV,2,1 n. 119(1152 Juli)

TEI Turtle JSON

Wibald (von Stablo) führt bei Kaiser Friedrich Klage über die Gewalttaten seiner Lehensleute (homines nostri, qui nobis fidelitatem iuraverunt et magna beneficia a nobis habent) Folkwin und Widekind (von Schwalenberg), die die zu Corvey gehörende Stadt Höxter (oppidum nostrum Huxariam) in seiner Abwesenheit überfallen und durch drei Tage hindurch verwüstet, dadurch einen Schaden von etwa 900 Pfund Pfennigen angerichtet, von den vornehmeren Gefangenen 253 Pfund erpreßt und als Vögte dieses Ortes die mit Erlaubnis Konrads III. angelegten Befestigungen (vallum et munitiones) zerstört haben, worauf die Corveyer Mönche den Gottesdienst und das Läuten der Glocken eingestellt haben. Er bittet ihn daher gemeinsam mit den Mönchen und Hintersassen des Klosters Corvey, diese Untat (hanc insignem contumeliam) zu bestrafen, da er sonst weder ihm und dem Reich dienen noch ehrenvoll in Sachsen leben könne.

Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The extortion is part of Wibald's complaint, typed rv:Payment. The passage supports that the complaint reports it, and the qualifier erpreßt keeps the nature; but the factoid stands as an act without any link to the complaint that alleges it. The same holds for the vassalage, advocacy and possession factoids, which are less problematic.
  • Finding rf:ground "Gewalttaten" names the subject of the complaint rather than a reason the regest gives for it. Acceptable but stretches the property.
  • Finding The damage of about 900 Pfund Pfennigen is an economic statement of the passage; the vocabulary has no act type for damage, so the omission is a model gap rather than an extraction error. The same applies to the destruction of the fortifications by the advocates.
  • Adjudication, recorded as a gap in the model Every statement after 'führt bei Kaiser Friedrich Klage über' is reported inside the complaint. The contract has no means to mark a factoid as an allegation. The qualifier 'erpreßt' keeps the nature of the payment. No correction.
  • Adjudication, kept 'Klage über die Gewalttaten' gives the violence as what the complaint is brought for, which falls within 'a reason the regest gives for the act'. The wording is exact.
  • Adjudication, recorded as a gap in the model 'einen Schaden von etwa 900 Pfund Pfennigen angerichtet' and 'die ... Befestigungen ... zerstört' need an act type for damage or destruction, which the vocabulary lacks. The extractor reported it.
  • Gap in the model, extraction Damage of 'etwa 900 Pfund Pfennigen' and the devastation and destruction of the fortifications have no act type (no damage or violence type); omitted
  • Gap in the model, extraction Extortion of 253 Pfund is typed rv:Payment with qualifier 'erpreßt'; the model has no type for a forced exaction, and all acts of Folkwin and Widekind are reported inside Wibald's complaint, which the model cannot mark as an allegation
  • Gap in the model, extraction Permission of Konrad III for the fortifications typed rv:Grant; recipient unstated and no right type for a fortification right
  • Gap in the model, extraction Monks' suspension of services and bells is out of scope
  • Gap in the model, review No act type for damage or destruction of property, so the 900 Pfund damage and the destruction of the fortifications cannot be recorded.
  • Gap in the model, review No means to mark factoids as allegations reported within a complaint.
  • Gap in the model, review Konrad's permission has no stated recipient; rv:Grant without recipient is the closest available form.
  • Open identity Corvey (RI004-002-C000961) is a register place entry for the monastery; used as community IRI for the possessor of Höxter
  • Open identity Wibald in the request is named only by the pronoun 'Er'; Friedrich only by 'ihn'
