RI IV,2,1 n. 132(1152) September 20Segni

TEI Turtle JSON

Papst Eugen (III.) trägt Abt (Wibald) von Corvey auf, die Angelegenheit des Bischofs Adelgoz von Chur bei König Friedrich zu fördern, gibt seiner Bestürzung über den von den Laien gefaßten Beschluß des Ulmer Hoftages (Reg. 110) Ausdruck, daß bei der Bestrafung von Raub und Brandstiftung an kirchlichen Gütern der weltliche Rechtsspruch der Exkommunikation vorauszugehen habe (novo iudicio excommunicatos dedicant, nisi prius in laicorum iudicio damnationis sententia feriantur) und berichtet ihm von einer auf die Anstiftung des Ketzers Arnold (von Brescia) zurückgehenden Verschwörung in Rom, wo für den 1. November die Bildung eines neuen Regiments und auch die Wahl eines Kaisers geplant sei, worüber Wibald König Friedrich vertraulich unterrichten solle, damit dieser die nötigen Anordnungen treffen könne.

Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The second mandate (Wibald is to inform the king confidentially about the conspiracy) is omitted although it is of exactly the same kind as the extracted first mandate and the contract can express it as rv:Appointment or rv:Request with Eugen as Grantor, Wibald as Recipient and Friedrich as Addressee. The preceding report to Wibald about the planned new regime and imperial election in Rome, instigated by Arnold of Brescia (candidate A001236), could be a rv:Communication; the planned acts themselves stay unasserted ('geplant sei').
  • Finding King Friedrich is recorded as rv:Addressee of the mandate, although the mandate is addressed to Wibald and the king is only the person before whom the matter is to be promoted. The extractor's comment acknowledges this; no participation is asserted.
  • Adjudication, corrected conservatively 'und berichtet ihm von einer auf die Anstiftung des Ketzers Arnold (von Brescia) zurückgehenden Verschwörung in Rom, ..., worüber Wibald König Friedrich vertraulich unterrichten solle, damit dieser die nötigen Anordnungen treffen könne' is about half of the regest. The mandate is the same kind of act as the extracted first mandate and expressible as rv:Appointment; the report is expressible as rv:Communication. The conspiracy, the planned regime and the planned imperial election ('geplant sei') stay unasserted, and Arnold is not referenced, because no role exists for the subject of a report and a reference would suggest participation in the letter (model gap).
  • Adjudication, kept rv:Addressee asserts no participation and is the nearest role for the person before whom Wibald is to act ('bei König Friedrich zu fördern'); the role for the target of an intercession is a model gap.
  • Correction Added <urn:regesta:22759:report-conspiracy> (rv:Communication; pope rv:Participant, Wibald rv:Addressee) with the two selectors named by the adjudication; producer correction-g7.
  • Correction Added <urn:regesta:22759:mandate-inform-king> (rv:Appointment; pope rv:Grantor, Wibald rv:Recipient, König Friedrich rv:Addressee; rf:qualifier "vertraulich"; rf:ground "damit dieser die nötigen Anordnungen treffen könne"); producer correction-g7.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No sender or author role for a letter; Pope Eugen recorded as rv:Participant in the communication.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No class for an assembly such as the Ulmer Hoftag; it appears only in evidence.
  • Gap in the model, extraction The reported Roman conspiracy, planned imperial election and the instruction to inform Friedrich confidentially were judged political and out of scope.
  • Gap in the model, review No role for the target of an intercession.
  • Gap in the model, review No sender role for a Communication.
  • Gap in the model, review No way to record a planned but unperformed act (conspiracy, planned election) other than omitting it.
