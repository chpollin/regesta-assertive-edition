Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The passage says only that Friedrich acts 'nach dem Vorgang' of Heinrich (IV.) and Lothar (III.); it does not say whether the emperors granted or themselves confirmed the exemption. Typing both precedent factoids as rv:Grant with the emperors as Grantor asserts a grant the passage does not state. The same applies to urn:regesta:22772:precedent-lothar.
  • Finding The object of the confirmation is the exemption of the monastery's people from all dues, but rf:concerns points only to the place Treviglio. The exemption itself is not represented as a right.
  • Adjudication, recorded as a gap in the model The passage 'nach dem Vorgang der Kaiser Heinrich (IV.) und Lothar (III.) die Befreiung ... von allen Abgaben' attests earlier acts about the exemption but not their kind. The candidate already keeps rf:qualifier 'nach dem Vorgang' and comments the unresolved reading; the act scheme has no neutral type for an earlier act of unstated kind, and rv:Confirmation would assert just as much as rv:Grant. No correction; gap recorded.
  • Adjudication, corrected conservatively The confirmed object is 'die Befreiung der Klosterleute zu Treviglio (Triuillio, qui dicitur Grasso) von allen Abgaben'. The contract can express it as a crm:E30_Right; since the vocabulary has no exemption type, only the top concept rv:UsageRights is safe, as used for the gaulum exemption in 22780.
