RI IV,2,1 n. 1371152 Oktober 16Würzburg

TEI Turtle JSON

Friedrich bestätigt dem Abt Hugo von S. Remi zu Reims (monasterii sancti Remigii in suburbio Remorum siti) nach Einsicht in die durch Abt Hugo vorgelegten Privilegien seiner Vorgänger Karl, Otto, Heinrich und seines Oheims Konrad, den in seinem Reich gelegenen Hof Kusel (curtem quandam Cosla vocatam infra regni nostri terminos constitutam) mit seinen namentlich angeführten Zugehörungen (Capellam, Glannam, Concham, Osterna, Querenbach et in comitatu de Kiriberch villam, que dicitur Brucca) und stellt ihm Güter in Thüringen (in ducatu Turingorum), im Ripuariergau (in pago Ribuariense), im Gau an der Rossel (in pago Roslisse) und das vom früheren Vogt (von Kusel) und jetzigen Mönch des Klosters, Albert, geschenkte Eigengut in Boppard (Buppura) zurück. Z.: Erzbischof Hartwig von Bremen, die Bischöfe Gebhard (Gebahardus) von Würzburg, Gunther von Speyer, Anselm von Havelberg, Eberhard (Ebrahardus) von Bamberg, Heinrich von Regensburg, Ardicio(!)von Vercelli, Abt Markward von Fulda, die Herzoge Heinrich von Sachsen, Welf von Spoleto, Markgraf von Tuszien, die Markgrafen Otakar von Steier, Albrecht von Sachsen, Konrad von Sachsen (Wettin). - Arnoldus canc. vice Henrici Maguntini archiep. et archicanc. ; VUU.: DD. O. III. 122 (VU. I) und Karl III. 106 (VU. II), verfaßt und geschrieben von A II. C. Quoniam regię dignitatis.

Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The restitution is a coordinated act of its own, not a provision of the confirmation of Hof Kusel; rf:partOf overstates the dependency.
  • Finding Abt Hugo's act of presenting the privileges and the monastery as their recipient are not referenced; the grantor readings of Karl, Otto, Heinrich and Konrad are supported by the genitive of the predecessors.
  • Adjudication, kept The guidelines treat dispositive clauses of one charter as provisions of its main act, and the run applies this throughout (22765, 22767, 22770). The link asserts no more than membership in the same charter.
  • Adjudication, finding rejected In 'nach Einsicht in die durch Abt Hugo vorgelegten Privilegien seiner Vorgänger' Hugo presents the privileges now; he takes no part in the earlier grants of Karl, Otto, Heinrich and Konrad. A reference on that factoid would misplace him.
  • Gap in the model, extraction The content and kind of the predecessors' privileges (Karl, Otto, Heinrich, Konrad) are not stated; recorded as generic rv:Grant, and the relation 'nach Einsicht in' (inspection as basis of the confirmation) is approximated with rf:refersToAct.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No property for a former office distinct from a current one other than rf:qualifier 'früheren'; Albert's status as monk was not recorded.
  • Gap in the model, review No state type for membership in a monastic community (jetziger Mönch des Klosters).
  • Open identity Heinrich among the predecessors identified with RI004-002-H000359 (Kaiser, welcher?); the register itself leaves the individual open.
  • Open identity The recipient of Albert's donation of the Eigengut in Boppard is not named; no recipient reference was set.
