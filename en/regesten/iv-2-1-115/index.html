Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The confirmation has two objects, the Besitzstand and the rechtliche Unterordnung unter die Abtei Cluny. Only the Besitzstand is a rf:concerns node; the subordination sits in rf:qualifier, which the ontology reserves for wording that qualifies type or time. A query over what was confirmed misses the subordination. The same applies to urn:regesta:22742:konrad-confirmation.
  • Finding The earlier act rests only on the comparative wie; the factoid carries no qualifier marking that Konrad's act is reported as a precedent. Acceptable, but the wording would make the inference visible.
  • Adjudication, recorded as a gap in the model 'den Besitzstand und die rechtliche Unterordnung unter die Abtei Cluny' names a legal status. A crm:E30_Right typed rv:ChurchRights would be a historical judgement the vocabulary does not support, and no state type covers monastic dependency. The qualifier keeps the exact wording, and the extractor reports the gap. No correction.
  • Adjudication, not required The rf:refersToAct link and the evidence 'wie Konrad (III.)' already carry the earlier act. The guidelines require a qualifier only for temporal words that are present, such as 'nachträglich'.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No state or right type for the legal subordination of a monastery under an abbey (rechtliche Unterordnung unter die Abtei Cluny); kept as rf:qualifier in exact wording with Cluny as Participant.
  • Gap in the model, extraction Konrad (III.)'s earlier confirmation is inferred from 'wie Konrad (III.)' as a prior act of the same content; the regest does not state it as a separate act explicitly.
  • Gap in the model, review No state or right type for the subordination of a monastery under an abbey (Cluniac dependency); rv:ChurchRights is only an approximation.
