Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding After "zwingt diesen" the possessive "seinem" can grammatically refer to Herzog Konrad as well as to Friedrich. The Child reference to Friedrich is anchored by a second selector "Friedrich dringt", which names Friedrich but does not establish that he is the possessor. The decisive disambiguation comes from the register labels ("Vater Friedrich Barbarossas", "Onkel Friedrich Barbarossas"), which the guidelines admit only for identification, not as facts. The same applies to urn:regesta:22645:kinship-oheim. The reading is very probably right, and the extractor flagged it, but the factoids state more than the passage alone secures.
  • Finding The regest states that the conquered castle belongs to the duke ("Burg des Herzogs", also "dessen Stammburg"). This is a possession relation, which the guidelines keep in scope even inside a military passage, and rv:Possession with Herzog Konrad as Possessor could express it with exact wording. It is not recorded.
  • Finding Friedrich is recorded as rv:Participant for compelling the request. This is the honest fallback, but Participant blurs that he is the coercing opponent, not a party to the request.
  • Adjudication, kept The ambiguity is real in 'zwingt diesen, vor seinem Vater (Friedrich von Schwaben) und seinem Oheim (König Konrad III.) zu erscheinen', but Friedrich is the grammatical subject of the whole regest, the parenthetical names identify the father and uncle by name and title with the packet candidates RI004-002-F000681 and RI004-002-K000316, and the extractor declared the reading as an unresolved identity in batch-5 and in a Turtle comment. The contract has no property to mark a contested antecedent inside the graph, and removing both factoids would discard a defensible reading. The ambiguity stays open for expert review through the batch report; a factoid records the producer's reading, not a fact.
  • Adjudication, not required In 'erobert eine für uneinnehmbar geltende Burg des Herzogs' and 'bis zu dessen Stammburg vor' the genitive and the possessive identify the targets of an advance and a conquest, which the guidelines exclude as military action. They are attributes of an out-of-scope act, not a central statement of possession, and the same decision is taken for the parallel genitive in 22637. The in-scope kinship and request factoids of the passage are recorded.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No role for the party who compels an act ('zwingt diesen ... um Frieden zu bitten'); Friedrich is recorded as rv:Participant in the request.
  • Gap in the model, extraction The state of feud ('während seiner Fehde mit Herzog Konrad von Zähringen') has no state or act type and is not recorded as its own factoid.
  • Gap in the model, review No role for a party who compels an act (coercion).
  • Gap in the model, review No act type for a feud (Fehde) as an extra-judicial conflict.
  • Open identity 'seinem Vater (Friedrich von Schwaben)' and 'seinem Oheim (König Konrad III.)' follow 'zwingt diesen'; the possessive is grammatically ambiguous between Friedrich and Herzog Konrad. Read as Friedrich's father and uncle on the strength of the editorial parentheses; the reviewer should check this reading.
