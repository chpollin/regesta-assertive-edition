Königswahl : Friedrich wird gemäß dem Reichsrecht (Ottonis Gesta Frid. l II cap. 1: nam id iuris Romani imperii apex, videlicet non per sanguinis propaginem descendere, sed per principum electionem reges creare, sibi tamquam ex singulari vendicat prerogativa) in Anwesenheit einer großen Zahl von Fürsten, auch aus Italien, unter Zustimmung aller zum König gewählt. Die Erzbischöfe Arnold II. von Köln und Hillin von Trier treten besonders für ihn ein. Als Grund für diese Wahl nennt Otto von Freising die Verwandtschaft Friedrichs mit Staufern und Welfen, die eben dazu geführt habe, daß die Fürsten ihn nicht regis Conradi zelo, sondern unter Bedachtnahme auf das Gemeinwohl dem kleinen Sohn des verstorbenen Königs, Friedrich, vorgezogen haben. Nach durchgeführter Wahl läßt sich Friedrich von allen anwesenden Fürsten Treueid und Mannschaft leisten.

Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The final sentence reports that all princes present swore fealty and did homage to Friedrich after the election. This establishes a lord-vassal relation and is in scope, but no factoid represents it.
  • Finding The regest explicitly attributes the reason for the election to Otto of Freising. As rf:ground of the election the reason appears as the regest's own, and the attribution is lost. The kinship StateFactoid rests on the same attributed passage.
  • Finding The preference of Friedrich over the young son of the deceased king, with the stated consideration of the common good, is not captured; only the parenthood is extracted from that clause.
  • Finding The child's nameInSource is the whole descriptive phrase rather than the naming wording.
  • Adjudication, recorded as a gap in the model 'läßt sich Friedrich von allen anwesenden Fürsten Treueid und Mannschaft leisten' states an oath act, but the contract has no act type for fealty or homage, and rv:FeudalRelationship is defined as holding something as a fief ('trägt etwas von einem anderen zu Lehen'), which the passage does not state. Building that state would overstate the passage.
  • Adjudication, corrected The regest gives the reason only as Otto's: 'Als Grund für diese Wahl nennt Otto von Freising die Verwandtschaft Friedrichs mit Staufern und Welfen'. The current ground 'die Verwandtschaft Friedrichs mit Staufern und Welfen' presents it as the regest's own. A longer exact literal keeps the attribution within the contract. The kinship state factoid stays unchanged; attributing a state to a narrative author has no property and remains a model gap.
  • Adjudication, recorded as a gap in the model The clause belongs to Otto's account of the reason ('die eben dazu geführt habe, daß die Fürsten ihn ... dem kleinen Sohn des verstorbenen Königs, Friedrich, vorgezogen haben'). The vocabulary has no role for a passed-over rival, and adding the son as Participant would misstate his part; the parenthood already extracted is the in-scope content of the clause.
  • Adjudication, kept The guidelines ask for the exact wording naming the actor in the passage; 'dem kleinen Sohn des verstorbenen Königs, Friedrich' is that wording and an exact substring, and it keeps the local son apart from the ruler, who is also named 'Friedrich'.
