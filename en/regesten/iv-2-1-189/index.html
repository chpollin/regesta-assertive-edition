Statements

An LLM-based agent generated these statements from the regest text. They have passed technical validation and have not been verified by a scholar. After two reviews they were corrected; the earlier state stands at the changed statement.

  1. Request: Petitioner: Bischof Stephan von Metz; Addressee: Abt Wibald von Stablo

    „Bischof Stephan von Metz bittet Abt Wibald von Stablo , daß er den König veranlassen möge, das ihm und der Kirche zugefügte Unrecht, das auch durch die Vermittlung Abt Bernhards von Clairvaux nicht aus der Welt geschafft sei, zu beseitigen“

  2. Dispute: Participant: Bischof Stephan von Metz; Participant: der Kirche; Mediator: Abt Bernhards von Clairvaux

    „das ihm und der Kirche zugefügte Unrecht, das auch durch die Vermittlung Abt Bernhards von Clairvaux nicht aus der Welt geschafft sei“

Transmission Epp. Wibaldi, ed. Jaffé, Mon. Corb. 551 no 413.

Commentary Bei dem erwähnten Unrecht handelt es sich um die Kämpfe in Lothringen zwischen den Metzern und ihren Gegnern, bei denen jene am 28. Februar 1153 eine schwere Niederlage erlitten hatten. Erzbischof Hillin von Trier bat Bernhard von Clairvaux um seine Vermittlung, mit der aber der Metzer offenbar nicht zufrieden war. Aus dem Todestag Bernhards (1153 August 20) ergibt sich der terminus ante quem. Vgl. dazu Simonsfeld, Jahrbücher 182 f. mit Anm. 116 sowie Parisse, La noblesse Lorraine 2, 717 f.

Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The passage reports a wrong inflicted on Stephan and the church and a failed mediation, not a dispute or suit before a court or ruler as rv:Dispute defines it. The injured parties are given rv:Participant, and the party that inflicted the wrong is unnamed. The typing as Dispute stretches the vocabulary.
  • Finding The request's content, that Wibald induce the king to remove the wrong, is lost: the king is not referenced and the selector stops before 'zu beseitigen'. The factoid states only that Stephan asks Wibald something about the wrong.
  • Finding The failure of Bernhard's mediation is visible only in the selector; no property records it.
  • Adjudication, kept 'das ihm und der Kirche zugefügte Unrecht, das auch durch die Vermittlung Abt Bernhards von Clairvaux nicht aus der Welt geschafft sei' reports a conflict that a mediator tried to settle and that is to be brought before the king; rv:Dispute ('Streit oder Klage') with Bernhard as rv:Mediator is the nearest type and asserts no verdict. The injured parties stay rv:Participant because the wrongdoer is unnamed. The missing type for a suffered injury and the missing outcome property are model gaps.
  • Adjudication, corrected conservatively The selector ends mid-clause and does not carry what is requested ('den König veranlassen möge ... zu beseitigen'). Extending it is safe. Adding the king as rv:Participant is not: he is the party whose intervention is sought, not a participant in the request, and the guidelines keep the addressed and the intended apart; the missing role is a model gap.
  • Correction Replaced the selector of <urn:regesta:22816:request> by the full request clause ending 'zu beseitigen'; producer of the request set to correction-g10. No reference for the king added.
  • Correction <urn:regesta:22816:dispute-unrecht> unchanged (adjudication: keep).
  • Gap in the model, extraction No act type for a suffered wrong (Unrecht) with a failed mediation; recorded as rv:Dispute with injured parties as rv:Participant and Bernhard as rv:Mediator. The failure ('nicht aus der Welt geschafft') is only in the evidence, no outcome property exists.
  • Gap in the model, extraction The king as intended target of the requested intervention ('den König') has no fitting role and is not referenced.
  • Gap in the model, review No act type for an injury or wrong suffered outside a formal dispute.
  • Gap in the model, review No role for the party whose intervention a request seeks (here the king).
  • Gap in the model, review No property for the outcome of a mediation.
