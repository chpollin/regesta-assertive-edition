Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding Konrad III's consent that Barbarossa take the cross is the act the regest is about. The candidate records only the background acts. The contract has no consent or vow type; forcing it into rv:Enactment or rv:Confirmation would misstate it, so the omission is defensible but should appear as a model gap.
  • Finding Entrusting the care of wife and young son is typed rv:Appointment with the content in rf:qualifier. Appointment covers 'Beauftragung mit einer Aufgabe', so the type is acceptable; an rv:Obligation state would equally fit. Agnes and Konrad as rv:Beneficiary is a reasonable reading of 'Obhut über'.
  • Finding Heir designation is typed rv:Enactment with an inheritance right labelled 'Erben seines Landes'. Acceptable given the vocabulary; the extractor's comment already names the gap.
  • Adjudication, recorded as a gap in the model 'weil dieser zugestimmt hat, dass sein Sohn Friedrich (Barbarossa) ... das Kreuz nimmt' needs a consent type and a crusade-vow type, neither of which the act scheme has; forcing it into rv:Enactment or rv:Confirmation would change its meaning. No correction; record consent and taking the cross as gaps.
