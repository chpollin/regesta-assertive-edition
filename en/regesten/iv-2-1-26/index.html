Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding The succession in the Herzogtum Schwaben is the main content of the regest and is omitted. The act scheme has no succession type, so the act itself is a model gap, but the contract could have recorded part of it: rv:Appointment is defined as Einsetzung in ein Amt, or a rf:StateFactoid rv:OfficeHolding with Friedrich as rv:Officeholder, rf:concerns the place Herzogtum Schwaben (candidate S000398) and rf:qualifier 'folgt ... nach' in exact wording. The father as predecessor would need a role the vocabulary lacks.
  • Adjudication, corrected conservatively 'Friedrich folgt seinem Vater, Herzog Friedrich II., im Herzogtum Schwaben nach.' is the whole regest; succeeding in a duchy is a legal relation in scope. There is no succession act type and no predecessor role, and rv:Appointment ('Einsetzung in ein Amt') would assert an appointing act the passage does not state. What the passage does state and the contract can express is that Friedrich holds the duchy: rv:OfficeHolding with rv:Officeholder. The father gets no reference in this factoid, because rv:Participant would claim involvement in Friedrich's office holding that the vocabulary cannot qualify as predecessorship. The place gets a regest-local node, as the guidelines prescribe for places, not the register candidate IRI as an actor.
