RI IV,2,1 n. 1961153 SeptemberRegensburg

TEI Turtle JSON

Friedrich lädt die Fürsten und Großen Bayerns nach Regensburg, kann aber wegen des Streites der beiden Herzoge (Heinrich von Bayern und Heinrich von Sachsen ) den Frieden in dieser Provinz nicht herstellen; da er nach seiner erst kürzlich erfolgten Scheidung (Reg. 167) an eine neue Heirat denkt, sendet er deshalb und wegen eines Vorgehens gegen Sizilien (pro Gwilhelmo Siculo, qui patre suo Rogerio noviter defuncto successerat, utriusque imperii invasore, debellando) Bischof Anselm von Havelberg und Graf Alexander (von Gravina) von Apulien (a Rogerio cum caeteris eiusdem provinciae nobilibus ob suspitionem dominandi propulsus) an den oströmischen Kaiser Manuel (ad Manuel Grecorum imperatorem ).

Review history of the LLM-based agents
  • Finding Friedrich, who sends the envoys, carries rv:Participant. The vocabulary has no sender role, but other records of this run give the sender of a letter or order rv:Grantor, so the sender role is handled inconsistently across the run.
  • Finding Heinrich von Bayern is left regest-local although the passage distinguishes him from Heinrich von Sachsen (H000470) as the second of two dukes, which leaves H000498 ("vorher von Bayern") as the only remaining packet candidate. The caution is defensible; record 22829 of the same run assigns H000498 to the same wording, so the two records diverge.
  • Finding The clause states that Wilhelm succeeded his deceased father Roger; only the parenthood is extracted, not the succession. The contract could carry it only approximately (OfficeHolding with qualifier), and the office is not named in the passage.
  • Finding The Latin side clause reports that Alexander was driven out by Roger on suspicion of seeking lordship. This is a political expulsion without an act type in the vocabulary, and it is peripheral to the regest.
  • Adjudication, recorded as a gap in the model The vocabulary has no sender role; rv:Participant is the contract's fallback, as adjudication g7 decided for 22754. No correction; the missing sender or initiator role is a gap.
  • Adjudication, corrected The passage names 'der beiden Herzoge (Heinrich von Bayern und Heinrich von Sachsen )'; H000470 ('Herzog von Bayern und Sachsen') is assigned to 'Heinrich von Sachsen', and H000498 ('Herzog von Österreich, vorher von Bayern') in the same packet matches 'Heinrich von Bayern' by name and title. The identification uses only this packet's candidates and aligns the record with 22829.
  • Adjudication, recorded as a gap in the model 'qui patre suo Rogerio noviter defuncto successerat' names no office, so an rv:OfficeHolding would supply one the passage does not state; the act scheme has no succession type. The parenthood is recorded.
  • Correction Replaced the local actor <urn:regesta:actor:22823:local:heinrich-von-bayern> by <urn:regesta:actor:RI004-002-H000498> (crm:E21_Person, label "Heinrich von Bayern") in its declaration and in the reference of <urn:regesta:22823:dispute-dukes>; role rv:Participant and nameInSource unchanged; removed the local node and its two comment lines; producer of dispute-dukes set to correction-g10.
  • Correction Divorce, mission and parenthood factoids unchanged.
  • Gap in the model, extraction No role for the sender of a mission; Friedrich is given rv:Participant.
  • Gap in the model, extraction Dispute parties are rv:Participant because the regest names no claimant or respondent.
  • Gap in the model, extraction The invitation of the Bavarian princes to Regensburg was judged out of scope (convocation, no stated legal relation); the expulsion of Alexander by Roger and Wilhelm's succession were not extracted as no fitting act type exists.
  • Gap in the model, review No sender or initiator role for a Mission; the sender falls back to rv:Participant.
  • Gap in the model, review No act type for succession or for expulsion from a territory.
  • Open identity 'Heinrich von Bayern' matches by title both RI004-002-H000498 ('vorher von Bayern') and RI004-002-H000470 ('Herzog von Bayern und Sachsen'); kept local. 'Heinrich von Sachsen' identified with H000470.
