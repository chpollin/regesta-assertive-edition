## 2026-11-18 Regesta without an exact day leave the line of the itinerary, imprint linked

The check judges the steps of that line and allows between two stations the most days their datings permit. A flag whose member hedges its day in the dating prose gets the class „Datierung im Wortlaut unsicher“.
