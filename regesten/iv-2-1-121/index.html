Prüfverlauf der LLM-basierten Agenten
  • Befund The regest reports a second act, the invitation of Wibald to a curia generalis at Würzburg. It is not military movement or travel but a summons, which the contract can express as rv:Communication with Wibald as rv:Addressee and Würzburg as a place. Whether a court summons is in scope is arguable, but no abstention is recorded for it.
  • Abwägung, nicht erforderlich 'lädt ihn für den 13. Oktober zu einem Hoftag (curiam generalem) nach Würzburg' is an invitation to attend an assembly and states no kinship, economic or legal relation. The guidelines put travel out of scope. The extractor recorded the scope decision in batch-30, which is the reviewer's own alternative.
  • Lücke im Modell, Extraktion No role for the wrongdoer whose deed a ruler promises to avenge; Folkwin and Widekind are referenced as rv:Participant in the assurance.
  • Lücke im Modell, Extraktion The attack on Höxter (Überfall) and the summons to the Hoftag at Würzburg are left out as out of scope (violence, assembly summons); the vocabulary has no act type for a summons to a court assembly.
  • Lücke im Modell, Review No role for the perpetrators of a wrong to be avenged; rv:Participant is a placeholder.
