Prüfverlauf der LLM-basierten Agenten
  • Befund rv:Judgment is defined as deciding a dispute by sentence, and rv:Judge and rv:Respondent are litigation roles. The regest reports that Friedrich has ministeriales hanged for breach of the peace; it names no dispute, trial or sentence. The candidate thus asserts a judicial form the passage does not support. The extractor marked this as a model gap, but the chosen type overreaches rather than approximates.
  • Befund 'seiner Ministerialen' states a dependency of the ministeriales on Friedrich. The vocabulary has no ministeriality or service-dependency state; rv:FeudalRelationship would overreach.
  • Abwägung, zurückhaltend korrigiert 'Friedrich läßt einige seiner Ministerialen wegen Störung des Landfriedens am Galgen hinrichten' names no dispute, trial or sentence, while rv:Judgment is defined as 'Entscheidung eines Streits durch Spruch' and Judge and Respondent are litigation roles. No act type of the contract expresses a punishment or execution, so any retyping would be a new reading. The extractor itself records the overreach as a model gap.
  • Abwägung, als Modelllücke vermerkt 'einige seiner Ministerialen' states a service dependency; the state scheme has no ministeriality type and rv:FeudalRelationship would overreach.
