Aussagen

Diese Aussagen hat ein LLM-basierter Agent aus dem Regesttext erzeugt. Sie sind technisch geprüft und fachlich nicht geprüft. Der extrahierte Stand vor den Reviews ist mit diesem gleich.

Prüfverlauf der LLM-basierten Agenten
  • Befund The main statement of the regest is the joint refusal by Friedrich and Konrad III. The candidate records only the request and puts the refusal passage into the request's evidence, so the graph does not say that the request was rejected. The contract has no refusal type; at most the claim before rulers could be read as rv:Dispute. The defect is mostly a model gap, but as it stands a query for this factoid returns an open request whose outcome the regest states.
  • Befund Ludwig VII. as rv:Mediator and Friedrich and Konrad as rv:Addressee are inferences. The regest says Odo wanted to achieve restitution through his king's mediation; it does not name the addressee of the request. The reading is plausible and the file marks it as the producer's reading.
  • Abwägung, als Modelllücke vermerkt 'Friedrich lehnt gemeinsam mit seinem Oheim, König Konrad III., die Restitution ... ab' is a refusal, for which the act scheme has no type; rv:Judgment or rv:Revocation would change the meaning. The first selector stays, because the rv:Addressee references for Friedrich and Konrad rest on that passage, as the guideline on a second selector for a role resting on a different passage allows. The file's comment already reports the refusal as a model gap, and no restitution or possession is asserted.
  • Abwägung, beibehalten 'die ... Odo von Deuil, durch die Vermittlung seines König erreichen wollte' supports Mediator for Ludwig VII.; the addressee reading follows from the same sentence, in which Friedrich and Konrad decline the restitution Odo sought. Ludwig stays regest-local because the packet has no candidate for him. The comment marks the addressee reading as the producer's.
  • Lücke im Modell, Extraktion No act type for a refusal or rejection of a request: the regest's main act, Friedrich and Konrad III. 'lehnt ... ab' the restitution, is captured only indirectly through the rv:Request factoid with them as addressees; neither restitution nor possession by any party is asserted.
  • Lücke im Modell, Extraktion Mediator reference uses the anaphoric wording 'seines König' as name in source; its identification with Ludwig VII. rests on the preceding phrase.
  • Lücke im Modell, Extraktion Odo's offices ('Kapellan König Ludwigs VII. von Frankreich und Mönch von St. Denis') are not extracted as rv:OfficeHolding because they are not needed for the claim.
  • Lücke im Modell, Review No act type for a refusal or rejection of a request (lehnt ... ab).
  • Lücke im Modell, Review Direction of the kinship term 'Oheim' is not expressible with two symmetric rv:Kin references.
