Prüfverlauf der LLM-basierten Agenten
  • Befund The guidelines exclude military action only unless the passage also states an economic or legal relation. 'Gütern König Konrads (III.)' states that the goods are Konrad's, which rv:Possession can carry with Konrad as rv:Possessor. The packet's candidate RI004-002-K000316 matches by name and title ('König', '(III.)'). The devastation itself stays out of scope.
  • Befund Both references carry rv:Kin, so the factoid does not say who is whose Oheim; the direction lives only in the possessive 'seinen' of the evidence. This is a vocabulary limit, not an extraction error.
  • Abwägung, nicht erforderlich In 'Friedrich unterstützt seinen Oheim, Herzog Welf, bei der Verwüstung von Gütern König Konrads (III.)' the genitive only identifies the object of the devastation, a military act that is out of scope. The passage states no act or state about Konrad's holding; whether the goods are royal or personal possession is a historical judgement the genitive does not settle. The contract admits a correction that adds a passage only where it is central, and this attribute is not; the extracted Oheim factoid covers the passage's in-scope relation.
  • Lücke im Modell, Extraktion rv:Kinship with two rv:Kin references loses the direction of 'Oheim' (Welf is uncle of Friedrich); only rf:qualifier "Oheim" carries it.
  • Lücke im Modell, Extraktion Unresolved reading: 'Verwüstung von Gütern König Konrads (III.)' is a military act without act type and was not extracted; the genitive could be read as a rv:Possession state of Konrad, but was left out because the passage reports devastation, not holding. Konrad would map to candidate RI004-002-K000316 if expert review wants the possession factoid.
  • Lücke im Modell, Review rv:Kin is undirected; asymmetric kinship words such as Oheim lose their direction.
  • Lücke im Modell, Review No act or state type for support or alliance ('unterstützt').
