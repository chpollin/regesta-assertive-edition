RI IV,2,1 n. 103a1152 Juni/JuliRegensburg

TEI Turtle JSON

Vor König Friedrich (coram rege Friderico) wird der Streit zwischen Eufemia, der Witwe Heinrichs von Hegilen, und dem Stift Berchtesgaden unter Propst Heinrich um Saline und Salzpfanne zu Reichenhall nach dem Zeugnis der Edelfreien Otto von Moos, Erbo von Biburg und Herbord von Hebingen durch Spruch des erwählten Richters Markgraf Engelbert von Kraiburg (Istrien) zugunsten des Stiftes entschieden. Nennung lokaler Zeugen, darunter Markgraf Engelbert von Kraiburg, Kuno von Mödling (Megelingen), Babo von Zulling (Zollingen), Pilgrim von Wolmotsa, Otto von Moos, Erbo von Biburg und Wernhard von Hornbach.

Prüfverlauf der LLM-basierten Agenten
  • Befund The judge is 'erwählten', chosen for this case, which distinguishes him from an office judge. The candidate drops the word. The contract could carry it as rf:qualifier.
  • Befund Friedrich is a Participant, which is the only available role for 'coram rege'. The passage does not say he decided, so not making him Judge is correct.
  • Befund Propst Heinrich is not referenced in the dispute, although the Stift is a party 'unter Propst Heinrich'. His office holding is recorded separately, so the information is present; a rv:Representative reference in the dispute would be optional.
  • Abwägung, korrigiert The passage reads 'durch Spruch des erwählten Richters Markgraf Engelbert von Kraiburg (Istrien)'. A chosen judge qualifies the kind of judgment (a decision by an elected judge, not an office court), which the vocabulary does not capture; rf:qualifier takes such exact wording, and the evidence selector already contains it.
  • Abwägung, nicht erforderlich 'dem Stift Berchtesgaden unter Propst Heinrich' names the head of the Stift at the time, not his acting for it in the dispute; his office is already recorded in urn:regesta:135064:provost-heinrich. The contract does not require a further reference.
  • Korrektur urn:regesta:135064:judgment: added rf:qualifier "erwählten Richters" beside "zugunsten des Stiftes"; producer set to correction-g11. Roles, evidence and all other factoids unchanged.
  • Lücke im Modell, Extraktion The regest names no claimant or respondent in 'Streit zwischen', so both parties are rv:Participant; the losing party of the judgment is not referenced.
  • Lücke im Modell, Extraktion The ruler's presence ('Vor König Friedrich') has no specific role; recorded as rv:Participant of the judgment.
  • Lücke im Modell, Extraktion rf:refersToAct is used from the judgment to the dispute it decides, which stretches its definition (later confirmation or assurance).
  • Lücke im Modell, Extraktion The formulaic list of local witnesses ('Nennung lokaler Zeugen') is skipped per guidelines.
  • Lücke im Modell, Review No role for the presiding authority before whom a case is decided (coram rege), distinct from Judge and Participant.
