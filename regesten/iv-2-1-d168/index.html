RI IV,2,1 n. D168

Vorheriges, RI IV,2,1 n. 167Nächstes, RI IV,2,1 n. 169

RI IV,2,1 n. D168(1153 März)(Konstanz)

Friedrich erläßt an die Mailänder nach Klagen der Bürger Albernardus Alamanus und Magister Homobonus von Lodi wegen der von ihnen gewaltsam vorgenommenen Verlegung eines Marktes der Lodesaner ein Mandat (iam dictum mercatum de burgo Placentino, in quo prius fieri solitum fuerat, abstulerunt et in quodam campo aperto, ubi nullus habitat, ipsum fieri constituerunt ac ipsos Laudenses ibi mercatum deinceps facere compulerunt).

Aussagen

Diese Aussagen hat ein LLM-basierter Agent aus dem Regesttext erzeugt. Sie sind technisch geprüft und fachlich nicht geprüft. Der extrahierte Stand vor den Reviews ist mit diesem gleich.

  1. Bestimmung durch Friedrich, als Adressat Mailänder, betrifft Marktrecht (Marktes der Lodesaner), bezieht sich auf Streit oder Klage.

    „Friedrich erläßt an die Mailänder“ … „ein Mandat“

  2. Streit oder Klage, als Kläger Albernardus Alamanus und Magister Homobonus von Lodi, als Beklagter Mailänder, betrifft Marktrecht (Marktes der Lodesaner).

    „nach Klagen der Bürger Albernardus Alamanus und Magister Homobonus von Lodi wegen der von ihnen gewaltsam vorgenommenen Verlegung eines Marktes der Lodesaner“

    Begründung laut Regest „wegen der von ihnen gewaltsam vorgenommenen Verlegung eines Marktes der Lodesaner“

Überlieferung Deperditum; erwähnt bei Otto Morena, ed. Güterbock, MG. SS rer. Germ. N. S. VII, 5 f.

Kommentar Nach Morena, a. a. O. 5 f. soll der Kanzler Arnold (cancellarium suum vocavit ac litteras, que sic dicerent, sicut Albernardus dixerat, eum statim facere precepit) dieses Mandat (litteris ... sigillatis sed tamen apertis) hergestellt haben, das der Legat Sicher überbrachte; vgl. auch Simonsfeld, Jahrbücher 172 sowie Reg. 192.

Prüfverlauf der LLM-basierten Agenten
  • Befund The Lodesans, whose market was moved and who were compelled to hold it in the open field, appear in no reference. They are the injured party of the dispute and the holders of the market right named by the passage. No community candidate exists in the packet, so a regest-local group would be needed.
  • Befund rf:refersToAct links the mandate to the complaints. The contract defines refersToAct for a later confirmation or assurance referring to an earlier act; a mandate issued upon complaints is a response, not a confirmation. The link is useful but stretches the definition.
  • Befund The Milanese are referenced as rv:Respondent via the pronoun 'ihnen'. The German sentence is grammatically ambiguous; the reading rests on the Latin quotation and on the mandate's address. It is plausible but is an interpretive step an expert should confirm.
  • Befund The market is typed crm:E30_Right with rv:Market. The passage speaks of moving a market (an event and place), not of a market privilege. The right reading is defensible but not stated by the regest.
  • Abwägung, beibehalten The passage names them only in the genitive of the concerned object ('Verlegung eines Marktes der Lodesaner'), and that wording is kept in the label of urn:regesta:22795:market-lodesaner. The regest does not say the complaint is made on their behalf (Beneficiary would be a reading), and a RightHolding state would rest on the right-reading of the market that the same review questions. The claimants who are named, the two citizens of Lodi, are referenced.
  • Abwägung, beibehalten The definition of rf:refersToAct names confirmation and assurance as examples ('such as'), not as an exhaustive list; a mandate issued 'nach Klagen der Bürger ...' refers to the earlier act reported by the same regest and implies no date.
  • Abwägung, beibehalten The reading is supported by the address of the mandate ('Friedrich erläßt an die Mailänder') and by the Latin narratio ('abstulerunt ... constituerunt ... compulerunt'), and rf:nameInSource "ihnen" keeps the pronoun visible. The reviewer proposes no change.
  • Abwägung, beibehalten The guidelines allow a right as concerned object, and the label keeps the source wording 'Marktes der Lodesaner'. Retyping it as a thing would be another reading of equal strength, not a correction.
  • Lücke im Modell, Extraktion No act type for a forcible seizure or relocation of a right (usurpation); the relocation of the market and the compulsion of the Laudenses (Latin quote) are not recorded as own factoids.
  • Lücke im Modell, Extraktion The content of the mandate is not stated in German; the Latin quote describes the wrong and is not used as evidence.
  • Lücke im Modell, Extraktion The genitive 'Marktes der Lodesaner' would suggest a right holding, not recorded because it rests only on the complaint.
  • Lücke im Modell, Review No relation for an act occasioned by another act (mandate upon complaint).
  • Lücke im Modell, Review The Latin narratio (removal from the burgus Placentinus, relocation to an uninhabited open field, compulsion of the Lodesans) can only be kept as an exact literal; the contract has no property for the content of a mandate or the former and new location of a moved market.
  • Offene Identität 'ihnen' in 'von ihnen gewaltsam vorgenommenen Verlegung' read as the Milanese (Respondent); grammatically it could refer to the complainants.
  • Offene Identität The Lodesaner have no register candidate; kept only in the label of the market right, not as an actor.
