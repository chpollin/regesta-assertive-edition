TEI Turtle JSON

Friedrich erhält von seinem auf den Tod erkrankten Oheim, König Konrad (III.), die Reichsinsignien (Otto v. Freising: regalia), und dieser vertraut ihm seinen noch kleinen Sohn Friedrich an, an dessen Nachfolge im Königreich er wegen seiner Jugend zweifelt, weshalb er seinen Neffen dazu für geeigneter ansieht. Konrad rät diesem, wegen seiner Nachfolge mit den Fürsten in Unterhandlungen zu treten (Chron. reg. Col.: Fridericum adhuc parvulum commendavit et ut pro regno sibi adquirendo principibus loqueretur suasit ).

Aussagen

Diese Aussagen hat ein LLM-basierter Agent aus dem Regesttext erzeugt. Sie sind technisch geprüft und fachlich nicht geprüft. Der extrahierte Stand vor den Reviews ist mit diesem gleich.

Prüfverlauf der LLM-basierten Agenten
  • Befund The regest's central political statement, that Konrad doubts his son's succession because of his youth and considers his nephew more suitable, has no factoid. As an assessment rather than an act or state it falls outside the act and state schemes, so this is mainly a model gap rather than an extraction error; but the ground wegen seiner Jugend could have been kept as rf:ground on a related factoid, and the file records the omission nowhere.
  • Befund Entrusting the young son is typed rv:Appointment (Beauftragung mit einer Aufgabe) with Konrad as rv:Grantor and Friedrich as rv:Recipient. This is a stretch the candidate flags itself; the son as rv:Participant is acceptable. Within the contract it is a reasonable provisional choice.
  • Befund Advice is typed rv:Request with Konrad as rv:Petitioner. rv:Petitioner is defined as the one at whose request an act happens, so it fits the advising party only loosely; the purpose wegen seiner Nachfolge is not kept. rv:Addressee for Friedrich and the refusal to infer that negotiations took place are correct.
  • Befund The handover of the insignia by the dying king is typed rv:Grant. The regest does not say whether a right is transferred or the objects are handed over; the type is acceptable, but rf:qualifier "auf den Tod erkrankten" would keep the circumstance that frames the act.
  • Abwägung, als Modelllücke vermerkt 'an dessen Nachfolge im Königreich er wegen seiner Jugend zweifelt, weshalb er seinen Neffen dazu für geeigneter ansieht' is an assessment, neither an act nor a state of the schemes. 'wegen seiner Jugend' is the reason for the doubt, not for the entrustment, so attaching it as rf:ground of the entrustment would misattribute it. The extractor records the gap in its batch report.
  • Abwägung, nicht erforderlich 'wegen seiner Nachfolge' states the subject of the advised negotiations, not a qualification of type or time, and it stays in the evidence. The contract does not require it as a literal.
  • Abwägung, nicht erforderlich 'von seinem auf den Tod erkrankten Oheim' describes Konrad's condition, not the type or time of the act, and is in the evidence already.
  • Lücke im Modell, Extraktion No entrustment or guardianship act type: Konrad entrusting his son to Friedrich is typed rv:Appointment provisionally.
  • Lücke im Modell, Extraktion No advice or counsel act type: 'Konrad rät diesem' is typed rv:Request with Petitioner and Addressee provisionally; the princes are not modelled as participants of the advice.
  • Lücke im Modell, Extraktion No right type for royal succession or kingship, so the advice and the doubt about the son's succession carry no rf:concerns; Konrad's doubt about his son's succession and his preference for his nephew is an opinion with no act or state type and was not recorded.
  • Lücke im Modell, Extraktion Kinship recorded once with both stated words (Oheim, Neffen) as qualifiers and two selectors; no direction of the relation is modelled.
  • Lücke im Modell, Review No type for a stated preference, doubt or designation regarding succession.
  • Lücke im Modell, Review No act type for entrusting a person into another's care (guardianship).
  • Lücke im Modell, Review No act type for advice or counsel distinct from a request.
