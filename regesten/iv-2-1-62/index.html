Aussagen

Diese Aussagen hat ein LLM-basierter Agent aus dem Regesttext erzeugt. Sie sind technisch geprüft und fachlich nicht geprüft. Der extrahierte Stand vor den Reviews ist mit diesem gleich.

Prüfverlauf der LLM-basierten Agenten
  • Befund The invitation of Wibald by the princes is an act the passage states, and the regest gives its reason (his recently finished legation to Italy). The candidate folds it into the evidence of the Negotiation factoid without representing the inviting princes or the reason.
  • Befund rf:qualifier repeats the object of the negotiation, which the factoid type and evidence already carry; the election as subject of the negotiation would be better stated with rf:concerns or left to the evidence.
  • Abwägung, nicht erforderlich The passage 'von den Fürsten (auch) wegen seiner erst neulich beendeten Legation nach Italien eingeladen' is a parenthetical invitation to negotiations; it states no kinship, economic or legal relation, the vocabulary has no invitation type, and a correction adds an omitted passage only where it is central and in scope.
  • Abwägung, beibehalten rf:qualifier holds exact wording that qualifies the type where the vocabulary does not capture it; 'an mehreren Verhandlungen über die Wahl eines neuen Königs' records the object of a rv:Negotiation that no concept names, and it is an exact substring.
  • Lücke im Modell, Extraktion No act type for a royal election; participation in negotiations about the election is recorded as rv:Negotiation, whose definition names a future agreement such as a marriage. Whether election talks fit that type is an expert question.
  • Lücke im Modell, Extraktion The invitation by the princes with its reason ('wegen seiner erst neulich beendeten Legation nach Italien') has no fitting act type and is left out.
  • Lücke im Modell, Review No act type for an invitation; rv:Request is the nearest concept but its roles (Petitioner, Addressee) do not name the inviting party cleanly.
  • Offene Identität 'den Fürsten' who invite Wibald are not modelled, because the invitation is no kinship, economic or legal act.
